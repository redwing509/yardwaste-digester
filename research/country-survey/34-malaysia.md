## Summary

Malaysian councils compost landscape waste and digest only food waste. Subang Jaya (MBSJ) composts "Sisa kebun penyelenggaraan lanskap" (garden waste from landscape maintenance) naturally, vermicomposts leaves, and runs a separate food-waste AD project; Ampang Jaya (MPAJ) has a food-waste AD plant ("OVER 100 tonnes of food waste ... diverted", 2024). The one garden-waste AD result opened is a 2025 UiTM Shah Alam student lab study: grass clippings "30.80 L/kg VS" of cumulative methane against "3.59 L/kg VS" for dried leaves over 46 days. No Malaysian yard-waste digester or released simulator was found.

## Verified findings

- **UiTM Shah Alam — garden-waste methane (lab)** — Mhd Basri N.S., Erne Mohd Yasim N.S., "Analyse Methane Generation from Garden Waste at UITM Shah Alam", *Junior Science Communications* Vol 57 2026 (Colloquium on Applied Sciences 2, July 2025) (https://journal.uitm.edu.my/index.php/JSC/article/download/7630/6363/58559): "A 46-day anaerobic digestion was conducted using a laboratory-scale reactor setup"; "grass clippings yielded a significantly higher cumulative methane volume of 30.80 L/kg VS, whereas dried leaves produced only 3.59 L/kg VS"; "k ... 0.0516 day⁻¹ for grass clippings and 0.0146 day⁻¹ for dried leaves"; "ultimate methane yield (L₀) was slightly higher for grass clippings (0.7106 L/kg VS) than for dried leaves (0.6945 L/kg VS)". Caution: the stated L₀ is lower than the stated cumulative yield, so the abstract is internally inconsistent; treat the numbers as indicative only. Both values are far below typical grass BMPs elsewhere in this survey.
- **Subang Jaya (MBSJ) — Pusat Biomass Bersepadu** — MBSJ, "Info Projek Pengasingan Sisa MBSJ" (https://www.mbsj.gov.my/ms/info-projek-pengasingan-sisa-mbsj): "Kompos Kebun Sisa kebun penyelenggaraan lanskap kawasan MBSJ diproses dan dikompos secara semulajadi"; "Kompos Vermi Sisa daun daripada aktiviti pembersihan kawasan dan sisa sayuran pasar dihancurkan dan dikompos menggunakan cacing jenis African Night Crawler"; "Projek Anaerobic Digestion (AD) Projek ini dijalankan dengan mengitar semula sisa makanan premis makanan, medan selera dan kantin sekolah bagi menjana tenaga elektrik"; site "4.2 ekar". Garden waste is composted; AD takes food waste. Tonnages: NOT FOUND.
- **Ampang Jaya (MPAJ) — food-waste AD plant** — The Star, "MPAJ saves 100 tonnes of food waste from landfills", 14 Nov 2024 (https://www.thestar.com.my/metro/metro-news/2024/11/14/mpaj-saves-100-tonnes-of-food-waste-from-landfills): "OVER 100 tonnes of food waste have been diverted from landfills since an anaerobic digester (AD) plant was launched in Ampang Jaya, Selangor, more than two years ago"; "processes food waste into liquid fertiliser and biogas".
