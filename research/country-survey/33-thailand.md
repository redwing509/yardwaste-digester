## Summary

Thailand has the most leaf-digestion lab work of this batch but no full-scale yard-waste digester was found. Rajamangala University of Technology Lanna (Chiang Mai) measured petiole-and-leaf waste (community mix, mango, teak) at only about 25 % of theoretical methane ("25.58%, 25.06%, and 25.38%"; one community mix "20.59%") (2012), and later found that leaves co-digested with food waste in two reactors gave "144.5 L, and 104 L" of biogas and methane, against "120.75 L, and 14 L" for food waste alone (2019). Valaya Alongkorn Rajabhat University co-digested community leaves with cow manure: "highest cumulative methane was 4,183 ml" at "64.61%" methane (2021). Bangkok's On Nut centre has a project to sort organics from mixed waste into biogas, "วันละไม่น้อยกว่า 300 ตัน" (at least 300 t/day), 65 % complete in FY2566 (2023); whether garden waste goes in: NOT FOUND.

## Verified findings

- **RMUTL — BMP of petiole-and-leaf waste** — Panyaping K., Kan-In W., Saenphrom S., Khampanyo A. (Rajamangala University of Technology Lanna, Chiang Mai), "Biochemical Methane Potential of Several Kinds of Petioles and Leaves Waste", *Journal of Community Development Research* 5(1), 2012 (https://www.journal.nu.ac.th/JCDR/article/download/282/289/): "%BMPs in the unit of the ratio of methane yield in system per methane yield in theory of the 1 (PLW of RMUTL), 3rd (PLW of mango), and 4th (PLW of tek) set were similar values; 25.58%, 25.06%, and 25.38%, respectively. %BMP of the 2nd set (PLW of Ban Tung Pee) was minimum 20.59%"; COD removal "59.72%, 55.00%, 56.61%, and 53.66%"; run "October 2011 - February, 2012"; waste:starter "70:30". Values are % of theoretical methane, not mL/g VS.
- **RMUTL — leaves + food waste, one vs two reactors** — Panyaping K., Maneesawang J., Boonsiri N., "Anaerobic Digestion of Mixed Leaf and Petiole Waste and Food Waste in Single and Two Anaerobic Reactors", *RMUTI Journal* 13(1), 2020 (published 24 Sep 2019) (https://ph01.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/rmutijo/article/view/192083): leaves "mango, lychee, and trek" with food waste "3 : 2"; "Co-digestion in two anaerobic reactors provided the highest biogas and methane yield 144.5 L, and 104 L, respectively. Single digestion in single reactor, that was control set using only food waste in digestion, provided the lowest biogas and methane yield 120.75 L, and 14 L"; "The hydraulic retention time was the same in 22 days"; "25 - 35 ºC". Totals per run.
- **VRU — community leaves + cow manure** — Thamwatchalangkoon P., Chintham S., Sawasdee V. (Valaya Alongkorn Rajabhat University), "ประสิทธิภาพการผลิตก๊าซชีวภาพโดยการหมักร่วมระหว่างใบไม้ในชุมชนและมูลวัว", *The Journal of Industrial Technology* (KMUTNB), DOI 10.14416/j.ind.tech.2021.08.001 (https://ojs.kmutnb.ac.th/index.php/joindtech/article/viewFile/5048/3685): "highest cumulative methane was 4,183 ml and the percentage of methane production was 64.61%", leaves:manure "1:1". Total per batch.
- **Bangkok On Nut — organics-to-biogas project** — กรุงเทพมหานคร project monitor, project 22000000-4253 (https://monitor.bangkok.go.th/mobile/myproject.php?pid=22000000-4253): "โครงการกำจัดมูลฝอยด้วยวิธีการคัดแยกอินทรีย์สารเข้ากระบวนการหมักก๊าซชีวภาพ เป็นเชื้อเพลิง เพื่อผลิตเป็นพลังงานไฟฟ้า … ศูนย์กำจัดมูลฝอยอ่อนนุช"; "(2566) ความคืบหน้าโครงการ ร้อยละ 65"; "ความสามารถในการกำจัดมูลฝอยวันละไม่น้อยกว่า 300 ตัน".
