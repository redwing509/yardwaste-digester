## Summary

Estonia's only digester for municipal biowaste is the EKT Ecobio plant at Maardu near Tallinn, opened on 29 November 2022 (about 14 million EUR, 3.9 million EUR ERDF support via KIK), designed for about 20 000 t a year and about 2 million m³ of biomethane a year for Tallinn's gas buses; in 2023 it actually made about 600 000 m³ of biomethane from nearly 10 000 t of household biowaste. It is a kitchen and catering-waste plant: the operator's own 2023 guide says branches and autumn leaves are not accepted and belong in composting, and the national animal-by-products register lists its feedstock as catering waste only. Yard waste in Estonia is composted — about 23 000 t a year of garden and landscaping waste was collected separately (2019, operator figure), and Tallinn-area garden waste goes to windrow composting at the Jõelähtme recycling centre. The other approved biogas plants (Aravete, Oisu, Vinni, Ilmatsalu, Ebavere, Laatre and others) run on manure and dairy/food residues. The closest Estonian methane data for green biomass are for semi-natural grassland and coastal-meadow plants (EMÜ).

## Verified findings

- **Maardu plant (EKT Ecobio)** — Ärileht/Delfi, 29.11.2022 (https://arileht.delfi.ee/artikkel/120106562/maardu-uus-biogaasitehas-hakkab-tootma-gaasi-tallinna-linnaliinibussidele): opened that day; "kasutab biogaasi tootmiseks üksnes kodumajapidamisest ja toitlustusest kogutud biojäätmeid kogumahus ca 20 000 tonni aastas"; ~2 million m³ biomethane/yr (up to 50 city buses); ~17 500 t/yr digestate for North-Estonian fields after certification; total cost 14 million EUR; KIK ERDF 3,9 million EUR; designed and built by Biowoima Finland OY, general construction Mapri Ehitus OÜ. Supplier (https://biovoima.com/en/reference/biogas-plant-maardu-estonia-2/, 10.06.2023): EUCO dry + COCCUS wet digestion technology, 30 GWh of biogas, "Feedstock 24 000 t/year: bio-waste from households and shops". Operator: digesters at 37–40 °C, 50–60 % methane (https://ecobio.ee/kaaritamine/).
- **What Maardu accepts** — Kalle Grents (EKT Ecobio), "Biojäätmed: mis, milleks, kuidas?", 28.02.2023 (https://keskkonnaamet.ee/media/6110/download): dry fermentation without process water; "Rohelised jäätmed – oksad, sügisesed puulehed* EI" (*"kompostimiseks sobib"); 10 kg of collected kitchen/canteen waste = 1 m³ biomethane. Register: OÜ EKT ECOBIO "algmaterjal: CATW" (https://pta.agri.ee/sites/default/files/documents/2026-07/ABP%20Loetelu%20PRIA%20EE%20nr_ga%2029.07.2026_VI.pdf, 29.07.2026).
- **Actual output** — Case Tallinn (Laura Hartikka, 2025, https://www.kiertoasuomesta.fi/en/tietopankki/tallinn-biogas-2/): 600,000 m³ biomethane in 2023 from nearly 10,000 t of household biowaste; 46 % of Tallinn gas-bus fuel. (This page and the EU handbook copy also say green landscaping waste "can be" fermented and date the start to 2019; both conflict with the operator and the 2022 opening.)
- **Yard waste goes to composting** — Eesti Jäätmehoolduskeskus, 2023-06-07 (https://www.ejhk.ee/et/uudised/raasiku-valla-biojaatmetest-tehakse-biogaasi-ning-komposti): Raasiku kitchen waste → Maardu; "Aia- ja haljastujäätmed viiakse … Tallinna Jäätmete Taaskasutuskeskusesse Jõelähtmel", shredded, mixed with wood chips, windrows covered with a membrane, 6-week process, certified compost. Volumes: ~13 500 t/yr kitchen/canteen waste and ~23 000 t/yr garden/landscaping waste collected separately; ~150 000 t/yr total biowaste incl. mixed waste (https://ecobio.ee/biojaatmed/; 2019 in the 2023 slides).
- **Farm biogas plants** — ABP register 29.07.2026 (link above): 12 approved plants; Aravete, Oisu, Biometaan (Koksvere), Ebavere, Laatre, Halinga, Viiratsi MANU; Tartu (Ilmatsalu) MANU + MIMC; Vinni MANU, DTC, MIMC, CATW; Kuressaare Veevärk and Estover MIMC. Aravete built 2012 and 2022, mainly cow slurry, ~35 000 MWh/yr (https://www.pikk.ee/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Henry-Uljas.pdf); Vinni, Ilmatsalu, Oisu since 2013, 45–46,6 GWh (https://rohegaasijaam.ee/firmast/).
- **Modelled potential (2014)** — Huopana et al., ABOWE (https://savonia.fi/abowefiles/reports/regional_modelling_reports/O.2.2_and_O.2.6_Heat_and_electricity_production_from_biodegradable_waste%2C_Lithuania_and_Estonia.pdf): MSW biodegradable fraction 180 GWh/yr theoretical; with an assumed 20 % availability 35 GWh/yr (39 kt FM); the optimum was three plants in the Tallinn region and one in Tartu. Biogas-electricity bonus 53.7 €/MWh (< 10 MW) at the time; landfill tax 10.0–30.1 €/t (2009), 29.84 €/t after 1.1.2015.
- **Pilot B (ABOWE, 2013–2014)** — https://savonia.fi/abowefiles/reports/newsletters/general/2014_2_ABOWE_newsletter_Pilot_B.pdf: mobile pilot at Kaarli Farm on liquid manure, mesophilic 40 °C; 57,2 % CH4; 6.6 m³ CH4/t raw material; samples analysed at the Institute of Agricultural Research and Tallinn University of Technology.
- **Green-biomass methane (lab)** — Melts, Heinsoo, Nurk, Pärn (EMÜ), Energy 61 (2013) 6–12 (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.energy.2013.06.016, abstract): late-harvested semi-natural grassland 269–299 lN CH4/kg VS. Mark (EMÜ BSc thesis 2021, http://hdl.handle.net/10492/6711): coastal-meadow plants 212 L CH4/kg TS, 235 L/kg VS, 55 % CH4.
- **Digestate research** — Kuusik, Pachel, Kuusik, Loigu (TalTech), Proc. Estonian Acad. Sci. 2017 (https://doi.org/10.3176/proc.2017.1.10): lab co-digestion digestates (catering waste, landfill compost, fish-farm sludge, glycerol + sewage sludge) — N, P, heavy metals within limits, Salmonella found.

## Not taken (unverified, contradicted or not Estonia)

- "Maardu processes yard and landscaping waste": contradicted by the operator and the register (kitchen/catering waste; branches and leaves to composting).
- "Environmental permit KL-511859, 20 000 t of kitchen and garden waste": not in the cited slides; not found.
- "Maardu is wet AD" / "the only dry-AD plant for green waste": the operator says dry fermentation; it does not take green waste.
- "Pilot B proves dry AD of green waste; University of Tartu took part": liquid manure; TalTech and the Institute of Agricultural Research did the lab work.
- "Current utilisation 20 % of 180 GWh": a model assumption.
- "Biomethane premium 53.7 €/MWh" and "landfill tax €30/t (2022)": a 2013 biogas-electricity bonus and 2009/2015 tax rates.
- "Aravete, Oisu, Tartu Biogaas planned, bio-waste feedstock": operating manure plants.
- "EMÜ co-digestion increases yield": the cited paper is TalTech's digestate-quality study.
- Wrong years/authors for five sources (corrected above).

NOT FOUND: an Estonian digester fed mainly with yard or park waste; Estonian methane data for municipal leaves or grass clippings (only grassland/meadow biomass); a released Estonian digester simulator.
