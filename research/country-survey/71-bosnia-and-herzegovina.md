## Summary

No source opened in this slice describes an operating dedicated yard- or garden-waste anaerobic digestion plant in Bosnia and Herzegovina. What was opened is more limited: a Federation of BiH waste plan that sets operational objectives for separately collecting garden and park bio-waste and for using biodegradable waste to make compost and biofuels; a Canton Sarajevo waste plan that gives separate-collection targets for biodegradable waste from gardens and parks and, in a later section, estimates biodegradable kitchen and garden waste and says that, because biodegradable waste is not collected separately, most of it goes to the Smiljevići landfill; a regional bio-waste guideline prepared for, among other cities, Bijeljina in Republika Srpska, which lists green (garden) waste among acceptable anaerobic-digestion feedstocks; and a University of Tuzla laboratory paper on anaerobic co-digestion of sewage sludge with kitchen waste and fruit-and-vegetable processing waste (not dedicated yard-waste AD). This slice does not cover the full legal, plant, yield, or simulator inventory that the complete country survey would require.

## Verified findings

- **FBiH waste plan, operational objective 2.3** — “Operativni cilj 2.3: Odvojeno prikupiti bio-otpad iz vrtova i parkova” (English translation: “Operational objective 2.3: Separately collect bio-waste from gardens and parks”). Official PDF of *Federalni plan upravljanja otpadom 2012–2017*, Federalno ministarstvo okoliša i turizma, opened as full text (HTTP 200). https://fmoit.gov.ba/wp-content/uploads/dokumenti/Federalni-plan-upravljanja-otpadom-2012-2017-Final.pdf
- **FBiH waste plan, operational objective 2.8** — “Operativni cilj 2.8:Biorazgradljivi otpad iskorišten za proizvodnju komposta i biogoriva” (English translation: “Operational objective 2.8: Biodegradable waste used for the production of compost and biofuels”). Same PDF. The colon in “2.8:Biorazgradljivi” has no following space on the page. The two objectives are separate bullets (2.4 sits between them) and are not quoted as one sentence.
- **Canton Sarajevo waste plan, table of separate-collection targets** — “Tabela 1: Operativni ciljevi za integrirani sistem” has the column header “Vremenski period” and the years “2011”, “2014”, and “2018”, and the row “Biorazgradivi otpad iz vrtova i parkova         30%                50%      70%”. Official PDF of *Plan upravljanja otpadom Kantona Sarajevo 2015. - 2020. godine*, opened as full text (HTTP 200). https://mkipgo.ks.gov.ba/sites/mkipgo.ks.gov.ba/files/2022-05/plan_upravljanja_otpadom_kantona_sarajevo_za_period_2015-2020.godina.pdf
- **Canton Sarajevo, estimated biodegradable kitchen and garden waste, and destination** — In section “5.3.9. Biorazgradivi otpad” the plan says: “Obzirom da se na području KS u prosjeku godišnje proizvede preko 201.684 tone/godišnje komunalnog otpada” and, after a page break, “i da 15% udjela čini biorazgradivi kuhinjski i vrtni otpad, dolazi se do podatka da se na području Kantona Sarajevo generiše preko 30.252,6 tona/godišnje biorazgradivog otpada. Pošto se biorazgradivi otpad ne prikuplja odvojeno, uglavnom završava na deponiji Smiljevići sa preostalim komunalnim otpadom.” (English translation: “Given that in the area of KS, on average, over 201,684 tonnes/year of municipal waste is produced annually, and that biodegradable kitchen and garden waste makes up a 15% share, the figure is reached that over 30,252.6 tonnes/year of biodegradable waste is generated in Canton Sarajevo. Because biodegradable waste is not collected separately, it mostly ends up at the Smiljevići landfill with the remaining municipal waste.”) Same PDF. This is a calculated kitchen-and-garden figure, not a garden-only tonnage, and it is later in the document than Tabela 1.
- **CCAC / SEE cities bio-waste guideline, Bijeljina as a named city** — “The Guideline for Bio-Waste Management was developed as part of expert assistance approved by the Climate and Clean Air Coalition (CCAC) in October 2019 for the cities of Bijeljina, Republika Srpska, Bosnia and Herzegovina, and Vrbas and Novi Sad, Serbia.” Official filename `6_se_europe_cities_organic_waste_mgt_guidance_final_draft.pdf`. The live CCAC URL returned HTTP 403 on 24 Nov 2026; the same official PDF was opened from the Internet Archive snapshot of that URL (2022-03-29). https://www.ccacoalition.org/sites/default/files/resources/6_se_europe_cities_organic_waste_mgt_guidance_final_draft.pdf · archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20220329011512id_/https://www.ccacoalition.org/sites/default/files/resources/6_se_europe_cities_organic_waste_mgt_guidance_final_draft.pdf
- **Same guideline, definition of bio-waste (conclusion)** — “Bio otpad uključuje isključivo otpad iz bašte i parkova, otpad od hrane iz domaćinstava, restorana, ugostiteljskih i maloprodajnih objekata, kao i sličan bio otpad koji se generiše u okviru procesa prerade hrane.” (English translation: “Bio-waste includes exclusively waste from gardens and parks, food waste from households, restaurants, catering and retail outlets, as well as similar bio-waste generated in food-processing.”)
- **Same guideline, anaerobic digestion and garden waste as a listed feedstock** — “Anaerobna digestija je biološka razgradnja organskog otpada u odsustvu kiseonika (anaerobnoj sredini), pri čemu se generišu metan i ugljen dioksid.” (English translation: “Anaerobic digestion is the biological breakdown of organic waste in the absence of oxygen (an anaerobic environment), which generates methane and carbon dioxide.”) Among listed acceptable materials: “Zeleni (baštenski) otpad” (English translation: “Green (garden) waste”). This is a regional guidance list, not a record of a plant operating in Bijeljina.
- **University of Tuzla laboratory co-digestion study** — Title printed on the opened PDF (across three lines): “ANAEROBNA KODIGESTIJA OTPADNOG MULJA IZ POSTROJENJA ZA PREČIŠĆAVANJE KOMUNALNIH OTPADNIH VODA I DRUGOG ORGANSKOG OTPADA.” Authors include an affiliation printed as “*Tehnološki fakultet Univerziteta u Tuzli, Univerzitetska 8, 75 000 Tuzla”. Quote: “U ovom radu ispitivana je anaerobna kodigestija otpadnog mulja sa postrojenja za prečišćavanje komunalnih otpadnih voda, kao osnovnog supstrata uz dodatak tri vrste organskog otpada (pileći ekskrementi, kuhinjski otpad i otpad od prerade voća i povrća) u cilju povećanja upotrebne vrijednosti otpadnog mulja kao sirovine za proizvodnju bioplina.” (English translation: “In this paper, anaerobic co-digestion of sewage sludge from a municipal wastewater treatment plant, as the main substrate with the addition of three types of organic waste (chicken manure, kitchen waste, and fruit and vegetable processing waste), was investigated in order to increase the useful value of sewage sludge as a raw material for biogas production.”) Official PDF, HTTP 200: https://www.voda.ba/uimages/Mirnesa%20Zohorovic%20i%20sar%20ZR.pdf This is sludge co-digestion, not dedicated yard-waste AD.
