# Denmark — yard-waste / green-waste / lignocellulosic anaerobic digestion (checked)

Searches in Danish (have-/parkaffald, haveaffald, bioforgasning, biogaspotentiale) and English. Denmark only. Every source below was opened; access level is stated.

## Summary

Denmark mostly composts or burns its garden/park waste (have-/parkaffald, HPA); only a small share was ever planned for digestion. The Danish EPA (Miljøstyrelsen) 2019 report puts total HPA at "850.000 tons/år" and quotes the 2013–2018 resource plan's expected effect for 2018 as "22 % til forbrænding og 3 % af haveaffaldet til bioforgasning". The one Danish full-scale digestion trial found co-pulped HPA with source-separated household food waste (KOD) in Gemidan's Ecogi line and recommended keeping HPA "under 25%" of the biopulp because recycling-centre HPA starts composting before treatment. Danish BMP data exist for garden plants (Aarhus/SDU, 2012). No Danish digester that takes mainly yard waste was found.

## Verified findings

- **National HPA volume and targets** — Miljøstyrelsen, *Have-/parkaffald til energiudnyttelse ved forbrænding*, Miljøprojekt nr. 2091, June 2019 (FORCE Technology), https://www2.mst.dk/Udgiv/publikationer/2019/06/978-87-7038-084-3.pdf (FULL PDF): "Den samlede mængde HPA i Danmark er opgjort til 850.000 tons/år. Hertil kan lægges en endnu ikke udnyttet mængde ”in situ”-fraktion på 150.000 tons"; resource plan 2013–2018 expected effect in 2018 "22 % til forbrænding og 3 % af haveaffaldet til bioforgasning"; "25 % af have-/parkaffaldet energiudnyttes i 2018 mod 4 % i dag (2011)". Municipal example (Herning): "den bløde, næringsrige andel skal udsorteres med henblik på bioforgasning fra 2018".
- **Full-scale co-pulping trial (Ecogi)** — Miljøstyrelsen, 15 Dec 2016, *Fremstilling af et højværdisubstrat til biogasproduktion ved sampulpning af have/parkaffald og kildesorteret organisk dagrenovation vha. Ecogi-teknologien* (https://mst.dk/publikationer/2016/december/fremstilling-af-et-hoejvaerdisubstrat-til-biogasproduktion-ved-sampulpning-af-haveparkaffald-og-kildesorteret-organisk-dagrenovation-vha-ecogi-teknologien; summary page): "muligt at behandle have/parkaffald sammen med KOD i Ecogi-anlægget, uden at der tabes mere end 14% af biomassen til rejekt"; recycling-centre HPA "begynder at kompostere så hurtigt, at det meste af det letomsættelige organiske materiale er omsat ... risiko for sediment i rør og tanke"; "andelen af have/parkaffald i biopulpen holdes under 25%". Report includes lab BMP tests and an LCA (full report not opened; numbers NOT FOUND here).
- **Funding record of the trial** — Grøn Projektbank (MUDP 2013), https://www.groenprojektbank.dk/mudp/2013/fremstilling-af-et-hoejvaerdisubrat-ved-sampulpning-af-havepark-affald-og-kildesorteret-organisk-dagrenovation: lead "KOMTEK MILJØ AF 2012 A/S", partners "I/S VESTFORBRÆNDING Danmarks Tekniske Universitet", budget "DKK 1.400.420,00".
- **Operator practice** — Gemidan, https://gemidan.dk/behandlingsanlaeg-til-madaffald-og-have-parkaffald/: food waste goes through Ecogi to "en ren biopulp til biogasproduktion", while garden/park waste is "behandles og sorteres til jordforbedring og biobrændsel" at six sites; Frederikssund site (from 1 Jan 2024) handles wood and garden waste "årligt ca. 100.000 – 110.000 ton".
- **Danish BMP of garden plants** — Triolo, Pedersen, Qu, Sommer, *Bioresource Technology* 125 (2012) 226–232, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biortech.2012.08.079 (DOI resolves; abstract on Aarhus University Pure, https://pure.au.dk/portal/en/publications/biochemical-methane-potential-and-anaerobic-biodegradability-of-n/): 57 municipal plant-waste samples; "The BMP of herbaceous garden plants (332.7 CH4 NL kg VS1)"; wild grassland "214.0"; "199.9 and 172.0 CH4 NL kg VS1 for hedge cuttings and woody cuttings, respectively"; lignin "100 g kg VS1 is the critical biodegradability point".
- **DTU PhD on urban organic waste BMP** — Fitamo, *Systematic Quantification of Biogas Potential in Urban Organic Waste*, DTU Environment, 2017, https://orbit.dtu.dk/en/publications/c953736b-998f-4153-923e-17bde5b02a5e (abstract): "the methane potentials of 87 urban organic waste samples were determined" and a NIRS model predicts BMP "in fewer than 72 hours".
