## Summary

At RCERO Ljubljana, Slovenia's largest waste centre, separately collected biowaste is digested in a thermophilic horizontal fermenter ("55 °C"), while green cuttings are kept apart and used as the structural material for composting: "Mleti zeleni odrez, ki ga prav tako sprejemamo v sprejemnem skladišču, a ločeno od ločeno zbranih bioloških odpadkov" is dosed onto the dewatered digestate going to the composting tunnels. In 2024 RCERO took "33.042 ton bioloških odpadkov" and aims to hold biogas at "7.500.000 Nm3/leto". In the lab, University of Ljubljana (Kolbl Repinc, 2022) found that adding "5,1%" green cuttings to sewage sludge raised its methane potential by "7,5%" over sludge alone ("470 ± 17 mL CH4/g VS"). No Slovenian digester fed mainly with yard waste was found.
