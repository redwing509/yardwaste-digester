## Summary

Peru has no commercial or municipal anaerobic digester for yard, park or garden waste. The only documented digestion of real garden waste is a 5 m³ research reactor at the Universidad Nacional de Trujillo, fed with campus pruning and mowing waste. Municipal green waste that is treated at all is composted: SERPAR's Cahuide plant (Lima, since 2006), the Lima city compost centre at Parque Metropolitano Wiracocha, and Pueblo Libre district. In Alto Selva Alegre (Arequipa) a thesis counted 314 t of pruning waste a year, mostly dumped or burned, and chose composting over biogas. Lignocellulose pretreatment research (torrefaction, steam-explosion thermohydrolysis, biochar) is at the Universidad Católica de Santa María in Arequipa and uses grape-vine prunings. The 2016 waste law (DL 1278) puts valorisation, including energy recovery, ahead of disposal, but no biomethane rule was found.

## Verified findings

- **Only garden-waste digester (research pilot)** — Cabos Sanchez, Lezama Escobedo, Bardales Vásquez, Medina Vásquez & León Torres, Rev. Invest. Cienc. Agron. Vet. ALFA 7(21):583–597 (2023): a buried PVC tubular reactor, "con un volumen total de 5 m3 y una carga de trabajo del 70 % de la capacidad total", at "22° C", fed with Universidad Nacional de Trujillo campus garden/pruning waste (12 species; *Stenotaphrum secundatum*, *Aptenia cordifolia*, *Chenopodium murale* dominant) with bovine rumen inoculum; results "2.42 m3/día de biogás, 119.2 L/día de biol y 12.47 L/día de biosol". No per-tonne or per-g VS yield is given. https://portal.amelica.org/ameli/journal/540/5404632008/html/
- **District pruning-waste tonnage** — Cárdenas Vidal & Tejada Marín, UCSM thesis (2019): Alto Selva Alegre generates "26.17 ton/mes y 314.05 ton/año" of pruning waste from "109 fuentes de generación" (187,448.4 m²); it goes to "el botadero Quebrada Honda, en torrenteras y muchas veces son incinerados clandestinamente". Of biogas, pellets, biochar and composting, composting was rated best. https://repositorio.ucsm.edu.pe/items/2bff83da-0966-4b7e-8234-12b7f067a208
- **AD feasibility, Alto Selva Alegre (not built)** — Valdivia-Espinoza & Guardia-Muguruza (UTEC), South Sustainability 4(1) e074 (2023): "En 2020, se valorizó 69,31 toneladas de residuos orgánicos, que representan menos del 1 % de la generación total"; modelled AD plant LCOE 104,63 USD/MWh, 89,65 USD/MWh with a 3,45 USD/t fee; power sale only "a través de una nueva subasta RER". Feed is all district organic waste, not only yard waste. https://doi.org/10.21142/SS-0401-2023-e074
- **Grape-pruning pretreatment (Arequipa, UCSM)** — torrefaction at 240/260/280 °C before 37 °C batch AD (Diaz Vento, master's thesis 2021, https://repositorio.ucsm.edu.pe/bitstreams/679bdda6-4fa0-4e8d-8a82-85b7dea8a83b/download); steam-explosion thermohydrolysis for dry AD, VDI 4630, 210 mL vs 96 mL methane — the abstract also says "40.35%" more, which does not match (+119 %) (Zegarra-Paredes et al., Veritas 22(1):39–47, 2021, abstract only, https://doi.org/10.35286/veritas.v22i1.293); biochar additive "reduce eficientemente el contenido de CO2 y H2S hasta 22% y 0.78 ppm" with no yield effect (Diaz Vento et al., Rev. Invest. Altoandinas 24(4):278–288, 2022, https://doi.org/10.18271/ria.2022.423).
- **Green waste is composted** — SERPAR plant at Club Metropolitano Cahuide (about 776 m², operating since 2006) processes green waste from "19 parques y clubes metropolitanos" and "produce 30 toneladas mensuales" (https://www.serpar.gob.pe/noticias/serpar-parque-de-las-leyendas-molitalia-y-bonchona-intercambiaron-experiencias-sobre-compostaje/); Lima city compost centre at Parque Metropolitano Wiracocha, opened Nov 2023, for market and park/garden organics of Cercado de Lima (https://www.gob.pe/institucion/munilima/noticias/871593-aperturamos-centro-de-compostaje); Pueblo Libre composts about 10 t/month including grass clippings (22 Feb 2023, https://www.gob.pe/institucion/munipueblolibre-lima/noticias/716636-mas-de-10-toneladas-de-residuos-organicos-procesa-planta-de-compostaje-en-pueblo-libre); UNALM composting pilot for Miraflores green-area waste (2016, https://hdl.handle.net/20.500.12996/2251); Científica del Sur life-cycle thesis on Lima municipal green waste compares landfill, dump, compost and Kon-Tiki biochar, no AD (2021, https://hdl.handle.net/20.500.12805/2110).
- **Small-farm digesters (not yard waste)** — Castillo Alvarez et al. (UTP Lima + Univ. de Cienfuegos, Cuba), Energies 18:338 (2025), Chillón Valley: "30% organic waste and 70% bovine manure", "63.75 L CH4 /kg of organic matter". The paper's printed site coordinates (−70.06 W) look misprinted. https://doi.org/10.3390/en18020338
- **Policy** — DL 1278 (2016) Art. 37: valorisation "debe priorizarse frente a la disposición final" and includes "compostaje, valorización energética" (https://www.leyes.congreso.gob.pe/Documentos/DecretosLegislativos/01278.pdf). MINAM (16 May 2024): "En el Perú se genera anualmente un promedio de 8 450 715 toneladas de residuos sólidos municipales"; more than 148 500 t valorised; no yard-waste line (https://www.gob.pe/institucion/minam/noticias/955458-mas-de-148-500-toneladas-de-residuos-solidos-municipales-son-valorizados-en-el-pais).
