## Summary

Iceland has one industrial digester, GAJA (gas- og jarðgerðarstöð) at Álfsnes, run by SORPA bs. for the Reykjavík capital area. It is licensed for up to 30,000 t/year of organic household waste plus 10,000 t/year of liquid organic waste, and in 2025 it received 19,397 t and produced 658,788 Nm³ of methane and 2,323 t of compost. Hermes concluded that yard waste is not digested in Iceland, but SORPA's own sorting pages say otherwise: separately sorted **grass and leaves are taken to GAJA and made into methane and compost**, and GAJA accepts "garden waste — grass and hay" from businesses. Branches, trunks and roots are used as bulking agent in GAJA's composting, and only the mixed garden-waste bin goes to landfill cover. No tonnage of grass or leaves going into GAJA, and no measured methane yield for Icelandic grass or leaves, was found. The only numbers for the Reykjavík stream are from a 2021 Aspen Plus simulation (University of Cantabria and Reykjavík University), which modelled about 7,100–8,600 t/year of "lignocellulosic biomass" collected by SORPA. Two farm/aquaculture biogas plants are planned in South Iceland (Bláskógabyggð, using manure and greenhouse cuttings; Þorlákshöfn, in full EIA), and a biogas test rig for fish-farm sludge opened in 2026.

## Verified findings

- **Grass and leaves go to the digester (SORPA, "Gras og lauf")** — "Í flokkinn má einungis fara gras og lauf án aðskotahluta." "Efnið er flutt í GAJU, gas- og jarðgerðarstöð SORPU og er þar unnið í metangas og jarðvegsbæti (moltu)." (The material is taken to GAJA, SORPA's gas and composting plant, and made into methane gas and soil improver (compost).) https://www.sorpa.is/flokkun/gardaurgangur/grasoglauf/
- **GAJA intake list for businesses** — accepted categories include "Garðaúrgangur — Gras og hey" (garden waste — grass and hay) alongside separately collected food waste; "Lífræn efni sem falla til á höfuðborgarsvæðinu eru unnin í stöðinni í metangas og moltu." https://www.sorpa.is/fyrir-fyrirtaeki/mottokustadir-fyrir-fyrirtaeki/gaja-gas-og-jargerarsto/
- **Woody garden waste is compost bulking agent, mixed garden waste is landfill cover** — branches: "Efnið nýtist sem stoðefni í jarðgerð í GAJA" (used as bulking agent in composting at GAJA); same for trunks and roots. https://www.sorpa.is/flokkun/gardaurgangur/trjagreinar/ · https://www.sorpa.is/flokkun/gardaurgangur/trjabolir-og-raetur/ — the general "Garðaúrgangur" bin ("gras, lauf og trjágreinar"): "Efnið er notað sem yfirlag á urðunarstað SORPU í Álfsnesi" (used as cover at the Álfsnes landfill). https://www.sorpa.is/flokkun/gardaurgangur/gardaurgangur/
- **GAJA licence** — "SORPA bs. hefur heimild til að taka á móti allt að 30.000 tonnum á ári af lífrænum heimilisúrgangi og allt að 10.000 tonnum af fljótandi lífrænum heimilisúrgangi til gasvinnslu og jarðgerðar … Starfsleyfið gildir til 20. október 2036." https://ust.is/atvinnulif/mengandi-starfsemi/starfsleyfi/urgangur-og-efnamottaka/hofudborgarsvaedid/gas-og-jardgerdarstod-sorpu-alfsnesi-gaja/
- **GAJA 2025 output (SORPA annual report)** — "Móttekinn úrgangur 19.397 tonn Framleiðsla á metani 658.788 Nm3 Framleiðsla á moltu 2.323 tonn". Separate food-waste collection started in 2023; a packaging separator in full use from June 2025 lets that stream go to composting, where before it was used "aðeins … til gasgerðar". These are whole-plant figures, not a yard-waste yield. https://arsskyrsla2025.sorpa.is/starfsemi
- **GAJA design (engineer)** — "allt að 36.000 tonnum af heimilisúrgangi á ári"; reception, processing hall, "tíu þroskunarklefum fyrir lífrænan úrgang" plus gas tanks; technology from "Aikan/Solum AS í Danmörku". No temperature, solids content or retention time is printed. https://verkis.is/verkefni/idnadur/gas-og-jardgerdarstod-i-alfsnesi/
- **GAJA compost quality problems (2021)** — Stundin/Heimildin, 27 Sep 2021: "Úrgangurinn sem fer í GAJA inniheldur eingöngu 70% lífrænan úrgang"; SORPA's own figures gave lead 798 mg/kg (limit 120) and cadmium 29,7 mg/kg; "Frá 11. mars til 12. maí hefur Sorpa urðað yfir 267 tonn af moltu". This was before separate food-waste collection (2023). https://heimildin.is/chk97/6-milljarda-gas-og-jardgerdarstod-sorpu-uppfyllir-ekki-krofur-starfsleyfis/
- **Simulation of the Reykjavík stream** — Llano, Arce (University of Cantabria) & Finger (Reykjavík University), J. Chem. Technol. Biotechnol. 96(5):1333–1344 (2021). Aspen Plus v10, 16 scenarios at 55 °C, inlet 3–323 t/d, using SORPA data. SORPA collected 7,130–8,633 t/year of "lignocellulosic biomass (LCB)" in 2016–2019 (Table 1; the paper does not say what LCB is physically). Modelled results: "LCB residue provides the highest methane yields, reaching up to 106.0 mL/g VS (SC3)"; MSW gave "0.356 m3 of biogas per kg of dry waste in SC1"; "all the simulated scenarios are in the range of 0.09-0.11 m3 CH4/kg VS". Model outputs, not measurements. https://doi.org/10.1002/jctb.6654 (open accepted manuscript: http://hdl.handle.net/10902/23831)
- **Planned plant, Bláskógabyggð (Lífgas ehf., 2025)** — to use "mykju frá kúabúum og afskurð frá garðyrkjustöðvum" (dairy manure and greenhouse cuttings), "um 50 þ. tonnum af mykju og garðyrkjuúrgangi", "um 900 mkr fjárfestingu". Agricultural/horticultural, not municipal yard waste. https://orkidea.is/frettir/lifgas-ehf-rekstrarfelag-um-lifgas-og-aburdarvinnslu-stofnad/
- **Planned plant, Þorlákshöfn (2026)** — must "fara í fullt mat á umhverfisáhrifum" (full EIA); feedstock not stated. https://terraforming.is/is/lifgas-og-aburdarver-i-thorlakshofn-fer-i-umhverfismat/
- **Biogas test facility (Terraforming LIFE, Oct 2026)** — for fish-farm sludge "í bland við aðra hliðarstrauma, til dæmis svínamykju"; ReSource International with First Water. Not yard waste; no results yet. https://terraforming.is/is/tilraunaadstada-fyrir-lifgasrannsoknir-tekin-i-notkun-a-islandi/
- **University lab work** — Reykjavík University MSc (Christy, 2023): two mini reactors, 52 °C vs up to 62 °C, 188 days, feed "grain, onion skins, dog food, and manure"; stable hyper-thermophilic operation reported. Not yard waste. http://hdl.handle.net/1946/44791 — University of Iceland BSc (Þórisson, 2015): MEA amine upgrading column, to be tested "in the methane production facility at Sorpa in Álfsnes". http://hdl.handle.net/1946/21602
- **Policy** — Lög nr. 103/2021 define "Lífrænn úrgangur" to include "garðyrkjuúrgangur" (horticultural waste). https://www.althingi.is/altext/stjt/2021.103.html — Reglugerð nr. 803/2023 on waste treatment (scope article and home-composting definition). https://adverts.stjornartidindi.is/B_nr_803_2023.pdf
