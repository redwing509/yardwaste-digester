## Summary

No Pakistani digester running mainly on yard, park or street green waste was found. The only plant that names green waste is the Lahore Waste Management Company's Ichu Gill community biogas plant on Lahore's outskirts (Rs14.9 million, green waste plus animal refuse, about 200 households; Dawn, March 2015) — no capacity, yield or later status reported. The measured yard-waste data are lab work from Mehran University of Engineering & Technology (MUET), Jamshoro, on Hyderabad (Sindh) city waste: yard waste alone gave 304 NmL CH₄/g VS added in BMP tests, and an equal fruit + vegetable + yard waste mix gave the most methane (up to 0.56–0.62 L/g VS added for winter wastes; 0.530 L/g VS at 20-day HRT in a CSTR). A University of the Punjab LCA of Gulberg Town, Lahore, found that sending the 12 % residual fraction (mostly kitchen and yard waste) to biogasification instead of composting cuts that step's global-warming impact by 371 % (overall 18.5 % vs the improved-compost scenario). Recent Punjab "waste-to-value" biogas pilots at Lakhodair (2025) run on slaughter and sacrificial-animal waste, not yard waste. Pakistan's draft Green Taxonomy (2025) makes AD of source-separated organic waste directly eligible.

## Verified findings

- **Ichu Gill community biogas plant (green waste + animal refuse)** — Dawn, "Biogas plant in Ichu Gill", 29 March 2015 (https://www.dawn.com/news/1172589): built by the Lahore Waste Management Company (LWMC) from July 2014 at Rs14.9 million; "It would produce biogas from green waste and animal refuse being abundantly produced in the Ichu Gill"; to serve "almost 200 households"; called a "model plant", with plants in Dunyapur and Chiniot planned. No size, yield or operating data; current status not found.
- **Yard-waste BMP and tri-digestion (Hyderabad, Sindh)** — Safar, Muhammad, thesis "Exploitation of Energy Potential through Utilization of Municipal Solid Waste", Mehran UET Jamshoro, 2018 (PASTIC record and abstract only: http://repository.pastic.gov.pk/jspui/handle/123456789/11592): "The highest methane yield of FW and YW was achieved as 428 Nml g-1 VS added and 304 Nml g-1 VS added respectively by using BD [buffalo dung] as inoculum at ISR-5"; fruit:vegetable:yard 1:1:1 "530-621Nml/gmVS" (winter highest); CSTR optimum 0.530 L/g VS, VS removal 86 %, methane 76 % at HRT 20 days, OLR 2.5 kg VS/m³·d; biological treatment 1.13 MW per 100 t/day of Hyderabad MSW. Temperature not given in the abstract.
- **Seasonal mono/co/tri-digestion** — Korai M.S., Mahar R.B., Uqaili M.A. (all Mehran UET, Jamshoro), Waste Management 71 (2018) 461–473, doi 10.1016/j.wasman.2017.09.038 (abstract via PubMed 29033020; paywalled): 40-day mesophilic batch; individual fruit, vegetable and yard wastes 0.36–0.40 (summer) and 0.39–0.44 (winter) L/g VS; co-digestion 0.42–0.45 / 0.46–0.54; tri-digestion 0.46–0.53 / 0.56–0.62 L/g VS added; equal blending ratio best. Per-substrate yard-waste value not in the abstract.
- **Lahore LCA (EASETECH)** — Syeda A.B. (Department of Space Science, Punjab University), Jadoon A., Chaudhry M.N. (College of Earth and Environmental Sciences, University of the Punjab, Lahore), Sustainability 9(12) 2242 (2017), doi 10.3390/su9122242 (open access): Gulberg Town (851,709 people, 283,387 t MSW/yr; 68.34 % openly dumped). Scenario STS 2 sends "residual waste (12.08%) (mostly kitchen and yard waste) ... to a biogasification plant with digestate that is used on land for soil conditioning". "When compared with the composting process, the biogasification process gives 371% reduction in GW in the form of CO2-eq"; digestate on fields or gardens a further 7 %; overall 18.5 % lower than STS 1. Recycling first, biogasification second.
- **Policy** — Ministry of Climate Change, Pakistan Green Taxonomy (draft, Feb 2025; https://mocc.gov.pk/SiteImage/Misc/files/Draft_Pakistan_Green_Taxonomy_Feb2025.pdf), activity W3 "Biowaste treatment: anaerobic digestion": "In anaerobic digestion, microorganisms decompose organic matter in an environment without oxygen, transforming it into a less-polluting substance"; directly eligible if "The organic waste is separated at the source". Punjab EPA "Environmental Assessment Checklists and Guidelines — Solid Waste Management System" (https://epd.punjab.gov.pk/system/files/SGU-13%20Soid%20Waste%20Management-Final_0.pdf) lists environmental impacts and mitigation measures for "Biomethanation / Anaerobic Digestion" facilities; it defines biodegradable waste to include garden waste. No AD-economics text.
- **Punjab waste-to-value biogas (animal waste — demarcation)** — Dawn, 29 July 2025 (https://www.dawn.com/news/1927342): biogas from animal waste at Lakhodair "for the first time"; "some 20,000kg of gas could be produced from 1,000 metric tonnes of waste", Rs6 million from sacrificial-animal waste. The Nation, 29 July 2025: "20,000 to 25,000 kilograms of biogas ... from 1,000 metric tons of waste, generating revenue of Rs 6 to 7 million from sacrificial animal waste"; Rs 190 million/yr recycling park. Express Tribune, 13 Aug 2025: abattoir-waste pilot "with a generation capacity of 25,000 kilogrammes of biogas per day". LCCI, 22 July 2025: Lakhodair waste-site redevelopment to give "approximately 20 tons of biogas per day" and Rs 1 billion/yr carbon credits.
- **Lahore organic waste (context)** — TUNZA Eco Generation ambassador report, Aug 2014: about 6,500 t/day of waste, 72.76 % organic (ISTAC 2011), "Approximately 4729.4 tonnes of organic waste"; Lahore Compost (Saif Group, from 2008) windrow-composts about 1,000 t/day, compost used by the Parks and Horticulture Authority. Composting, not AD; yard share not stated.

## Simulators and modeling tools

Simulators found after check: **EASETECH** (Environmental Assessment System for Environmental TECHnologies), Technical University of Denmark — waste-management LCA software with anaerobic-digestion, landfill-gas and land-application process modules. Licence: not open source; available only to trained users who sign a licence (free for academic users, fee for commercial users). Official page: http://www.easetech.dk/model-description. Pakistani use: Syeda et al., Sustainability 2017, doi 10.3390/su9122242 (Gulberg Town, Lahore). Not Pakistani-developed. No Pakistani-developed digester simulator found.
