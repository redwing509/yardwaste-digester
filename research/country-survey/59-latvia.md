## Summary

Latvia's biggest digester for municipal biowaste is the Getliņi EKO dry-fermentation tunnel complex near Riga, accepted into service on 6 April 2021 with a design capacity of 125 000 t a year of biowaste ("food and garden waste"); in 2022 it actually processed about 80 000 t a year, mostly organics mechanically separated from mixed waste (more than 6 000 t a month) rather than separately collected biowaste (about 600 t a month). Yard waste itself mostly goes to composting: the national waste plan says green-waste sites compost "only green garden and park waste", and the industry association says green garden waste should go to compost and kitchen waste to biogas. In the 2020 waste statistics about 14 600 t of garden and park waste (EWC 20 02 01) was used as fuel (R1). Latvia's roughly 50 farm biogas plants run on manure, crop waste and maize silage (wet, mesophilic). Latvian lab data for leaves and grass do exist (LBTU, 2024).

EUR. Permanent operation from 7 January 2022 (https://www.getlini.lv/getlini-eko-3/; biowaste includes mown grass, leaves, twigs). LSM, 2022-09-23 (https://www.lsm.lv/raksts/zinas/ekonomika/riga-un-pieriga-uzsakta-biologisko-atkritumu-skirosana-getlinu-poligona-no-tiem-razo-gazi-un-tehnisko-kompostu.a474853/): about 80 000 t/yr processed; 2 buildings × 16 tunnels; ~600 t/month source-separated vs >6 000 t/month from the sorting plant.
- **National waste plan 2021–2028** — MK rīkojums Nr. 45, 22 Jan 2021 (https://www.varam.gov.lv/lv/media/6191/download): separate biowaste collection in Pierīga from 1 Jan 2021 and the rest of Latvia by 2024; some municipalities already collect garden and park waste (leaves, branches, shrubs); green-waste composting sites take only garden and park waste; AD units for mechanically separated and separately collected biowaste planned at five landfills; Getliņi AD under construction with 100 tūkst. t/yr net capacity (p. 190); biomethane-upgrading units at landfills listed as investments.
- **Garden/park waste statistics (2020)** — LVĢMC "Nr.3-Atkritumi" summary (https://videscentrs.lvgmc.lv/files/Vide/Atkritumi_un_radiacijas_objekti/Nr_3_parskats_par_atkritumiem/3Atkritumi_kopsavilkums_2020.pdf), reporting organisations only: EWC 20 02 01 collected 38 116.34 t; treated R3D 32 246.90 t, R3A 12 866.86 t, R1 (fuel) 14 573.10 t, R12A 2 827.82 t; landfilled 3 705.32 t.
- **Farm biogas sector (2020)** — VVD report, 30.07.2021 (https://www.vvd.gov.lv/lv/media/9315/download?attachment=): 52 plants, 57.823 MWe, 305.6 GWh electricity (5 landfill plants 8.875 MWe); 47 digestate-producing plants processed 1.66 Mt (manure 47 %, crop waste 34 %, food industry 14 %, sludge 5 %; "green biomasses" i.e. crop green mass 8 %, corn silage 21 %); wet digestion ≤10 % DM at 37–40 °C; digestate 1.53 Mt with 2.94 kt P2O5 (mineral P need −8.6 %); no biomethane plants for transport in 2021.
- **Leaf and grass methane (lab)** — LBTU, ERDev 2024: maple leaves 0.262, birch leaves 0.219, miscanthus 0.236 L CH4 g-1 DOM, 38 °C batch (Dubrovskis & Dubrovskis, https://doi.org/10.22616/ERDev.2024.23.TF106); grass from green areas and roadsides 0.313 L gVS-1 after double freeze–thaw at −23 °C, twice the control (Plume et al., https://doi.org/10.22616/ERDev.2024.23.TF128). Vegetable wastes: pumpkin 0.422, marrow 0.274, apple 0.451 L g-1 DOM (Dubrovskis & Plume, Agronomy Research 2017, https://agronomy.emu.ee/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/Vol15Nr1_Dubrovskis.pdf).
- **Landfill gas** — Dubrovskis 2018 (https://lfg-baltic.beic.nu/Resources/P89_Vilis_Dubrovskis.pdf): 59 biogas plants built in Latvia, six at landfills; Getliņi 6,28 MWel. Landfill gas, not yard-waste AD.
- **Digestate handling** — RTU Water Research Laboratory, 2017 (https://johnnurmisensaatio.fi/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/latvia_biogas-risk-assessment_final.pdf): 60 plants, 61.156 MW; Sprūževa M and Vecsiljāņi separate digestate; Egg Energy and Jaundzelves may sell granulated digestate.
