## Summary

No UAE digester running on yard, park or street green waste was found, and no UAE lab test on urban grass or prunings. Green waste goes to composting: Dubai Municipality's free green-waste pickup (since 2014) turns garden waste into compost or mulch for parks, and a private Dubai firm (Tadweer Waste Treatments) makes "green compost" from grass, shrubs and leaves. The first integrated digestion pilot, by Emirates Biotech and UAE University in Al Ain, will take up to 40 kg a day of food waste and compostable packaging (not green waste), digest it and compost the digestate; it runs Aug 2026–Aug 2028, with commissioning by Aug 2027. Measured UAE work is on food waste (517.38 mL CH₄/g VS, Khalifa University thesis, 2014) and on date-palm fronds as a biorefinery feedstock (organosolv, Masdar Institute). An ASPEN PLUS model hosted by UAE universities found date-palm leaf gave the most methane but also more CO₂. Dubai's 2024 organic-waste guideline asks operators to consider digestion and counts yard trimmings as organic waste.

## Verified findings

- **Emirates Biotech + UAEU pilot (food waste, AD + composting)** — WAM, 22 Sep 2026 (https://www.wam.ae/en/article/c2dikyh-emirates-biotech-uaeu-launch-uae%E2%80%99s-first); same release on Emirates Biotech, 17 Sep 2026 (https://emiratesbiotech.com/emirates-biotech-and-united-arab-emirates-university-launch-uaes-first-integrated-organic-waste-valorization-pilot-plant/). Near the UAEU campus in Al Ain; up to 40 kg/day of mixed food waste with compostable packaging; "The pilot plant will integrate anaerobic digestion and composting, producing renewable biogas and nutrient-rich compost." Aug 2026–Aug 2028; commissioning by Aug 2027; lab test of biogas to hydrogen. If scaled, could cut CO₂ by 89 % vs landfill (press claim). Food waste is nearly 40 % of UAE daily MSW. UAEU project lead Prof. Zafar Said Sher.
- **Food waste and other Abu Dhabi wastes (lab BMP)** — Sowunmi A., "Anaerobic Digestion of Organic Wastes Generated in the Emirate of Abu Dhabi. A Biorefinery Approach", MSc thesis, Khalifa University (Chemical and Petroleum Engineering), 2014, supervisor J. Schmidt (https://khazna.ku.ac.ae/en/studentTheses/anaerobic-digestion-of-organic-wastes-generated-in-the-emirate-of). Nine wastes (food waste, camel/cattle/sheep/goat dung, animal blood, A4 paper, newspaper, carton), mesophilic batch; food waste best at 517.38 mL CH₄/g VS added (STP), newspaper worst; steam pretreatment (121 °C, 30 min) helped most dungs, blood and newspaper. No yard waste.
- **Date-palm fronds (biorefinery pretreatment, not AD)** — Cybulska I., Brudecki G.P., Zembrzuska J., Schmidt J.E., Lopez C.G.-B., Thomsen M.H. (Masdar Institute; Poznań University of Technology), "Organosolv delignification of agricultural residues (date palm fronds, Phoenix dactylifera L.) of the United Arab Emirates", Applied Energy 185 (2017) 1040–1050, doi 10.1016/j.apenergy.2016.01.094 (abstract: https://vbn.aau.dk/en/publications/organosolv-delignification-of-agricultural-residues-date-palm-fro/). Fronds are the "primary agricultural residue of the United Arab Emirates" (urban vs farm not stated). Lignin recovery rose only with temperature (140–200 °C); best 12.93 g lignin and 21.38 g glucose per 100 g raw material; xylan losses up to 70 %.
- **ASPEN PLUS co-digestion model (date palm leaf)** — Inayat A., Raza M., Ghenai C., Shanableh A., Said Z., Samman S., Al-Mansori A., Lazkani A., "Simulation of Anaerobic Co-Digestion Process for the Biogas Production using ASPEN PLUS", ASET 2019, Dubai, doi 10.1109/ICASET.2019.8714403 (record: https://research.uaeu.ac.ae/en/publications/simulation-of-anaerobic-co-digestion-process-for-the-biogas-produ/; at least one author at University of Sharjah). Date seed, date palm leaf, coffee waste, wastewater and manure; "The best biomass for maximum methane production is a dates tree leaf, but its CO2 emission is higher than the rest." Simulation only (abstract).
- **Dubai guideline** — Dubai Municipality, "Technical Guidelines on Organic Waste & Garbage Treatment", DM-WSPD-P04-029 v1.0, 22/08/2024 (https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2026/06/30-Technical-Guidelines-on-Organic-Waste-Garbage-Treatment.pdf). Biological treatment = composting (aerobic) and anaerobic digestion; "Consider the application of waste-to-energy or anaerobic digestion technologies"; thermophilic AD suggested for pathogen kill; yard trimmings counted as non-hazardous organic waste.
- **Green waste → compost (Dubai)** — Free Green Waste Removal Service, introduced 2014 by Dubai Municipality's Waste Management Department; garden waste "processed into compost or mulch, which is reused in Dubai's parks and landscaping projects" (secondary media, khaleejtip.com, 7 Nov 2025: https://khaleejtip.com/dubai-launches-free-green-waste-removal-service/). Tadweer Waste Treatments LLC (Dubai, private; https://tadweer.com/): "Green Compost is the product resulting from the controlled biological decomposition of selected local greens (Grass, shrubs, leaves, fruits & vegetables)"; a "Bio-Gas Project" is listed only as a future plan.
- **Policy** — Federal Law No. 12 of 2018 on Integrated Waste Management, issued 18 Dec 2018, effective 31 Mar 2019 (https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1164; text not shown on the page). UAE portal (https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/environment-and-energy/waste-management): Cabinet Resolution No. 39 sets its executive regulations; Dubai classes "green/horticultural waste" as its own stream (Dubai Integrated Waste Management Strategy 2021–2041); Tadweer Group (Abu Dhabi) aims to divert 80 % of waste from landfill by 2031; Ajman runs organic-waste treatment of 150 t/day (method not stated). No green-waste digestion rule or biomethane rule found.
