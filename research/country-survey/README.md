# Yard-waste digester — country survey

Literature research only. These notes collect **summaries, numbers, citations, and links** on anaerobic digestion and related processes for yard waste, green waste, garden and park cuttings, and other lignocellulosic feedstocks, one country at a time. They do **not** change digester model code, simulator installs, or plant designs.

Unverified, secondary, or unopened figures are marked in each note. Prefer the verification sections over raw draft text. When a source is paywalled, a legitimate alternate copy is used if one exists (official report, preprint, thesis, repository, or slides). Piracy sites are not used. Copyrighted PDFs are not committed here.

Each country note includes a short **Simulators and modeling tools** section. Tools listed there are candidates only; installing any of them still needs a separate OK.
