## Summary

No Spanish plant was found that digests mainly yard, park or pruning waste. Spanish municipal plants digest the organic fraction of household waste and use park pruning as a structuring agent for composting the digestate: at Ecopark 2 near Barcelona (Valorga, 3 digesters, 120,000 t/yr AD capacity, since 2003) "The green waste is mixed with the digested material from the anaerobic digestion prior to its introduction to the aerobic composting tunnels"; the plant makes 12.6 million m³ of biogas and 38,000 t of compost a year. Madrid's Pinto biomethanisation plant receives about 13 million kg/yr of pruning waste (13,148,360 kg in 2022), but whether any of it enters the digesters is not stated. Spanish yard-waste methane yields come only from laboratory BMP tests: Madrid park pruning lowers the yield of biowaste (biowaste alone 426 ± 16 mL CH4/g VS; 75 % biowaste + 25 % pruning 345 ± 4 mL CH4/g VS; UAM 2021), and grass clippings give 169 NmL CH4/g VS (BDI 41.2 %) against 453 for fruit-vegetable waste (Univ. of Málaga 2026). Nationally, Spain had 146 biogas plants (2.74 TWh/yr) and 6 biomethane plants in 2023, none fed with pruning; the Biogas Roadmap (March 2022) targets 10.4 TWh/yr of biogas from 2030.
