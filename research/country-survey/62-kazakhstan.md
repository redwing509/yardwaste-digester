## Summary

Kazakhstan has no anaerobic digester for yard or garden waste. Its few biogas plants run on manure, poultry litter or wastewater: the one biogas station selling power to the grid (per KEGOC) is the Agrofirma Kurma poultry farm (Karaganda region, 1.07 MW, 2018), and farm plants such as Karaman-K (Kostanay, since 2011) use manure. National statistics recorded only 16 513 t of "tree and foliage waste" collected in 2020. Cities mostly landfill it: Almaty sends collected leaves to the city landfill, Almaty oblast has no separate system for grass and branches, and Astana's landfill only stores waste. A planned Aktau MBT with anaerobic digestion (EBRD, 2012) would mix garden waste into its digestate compost. Kazakhstan has no laboratory methane data for grass or leaves.

## Verified findings

- **National biodegradable-waste picture** — Orazbekov Y. (SWITCH-Asia, for the Ministry of Ecology, Geology and Natural Resources), "Biodegradable Waste in the Republic of Kazakhstan", Nov 2022 (https://ftp.switch-asia.eu/site/assets/files/3607/biodegradable_waste_kazakhstan_en-1.pdf): "18,166 tonnes of Food Waste and 16,513 tonnes of Tree and Foliage Waste were collected in Kazakhstan in 2020"; MSW 4.6 Mt (2020) and 4.2 Mt (2021). The Environmental Code defines biodegradable waste as including "waste from gardens, parks, food, and cooking" (p. 11). It names one BDW station selling power: Agrofirma Kurma poultry farm, 1.07 MW, 2018, about 4 800 thousand kWh a year. The other plants it names are Shymkent water channel, the Volynsky pig farm and Karaman-K (0.35 MW, manure). Biopower plants: 7.82 MW and 6.6 million kWh in 2020. The renewables placement plan lists six planned biogas power plants totalling 17.817 MW. Support comes from fixed RES tariffs, biogas included (Government Decree No 925 of 29.12.2017). The Green Taxonomy (Decree No 996 of 31.12.2021) lists "bioenergy" under renewable energy.
- **Farm biogas plants** — Yerassyl et al., Energies 15(9) 3270, 2022, https://doi.org/10.3390/en15093270: Karaman-K farm (Kostanay) plant operating since 2011, equipment by "Zorg Biogas Ukraine", 2 × 2400 m³ reactors, 360 kW; Kogershin pig complex (Zhambyl) 5300 m³ biogas/day; manure-only national potential "2,495,006 m³ of methane or 4,620,633 m³ of biogas".
- **Aktau MBT + AD (planned)** — COWI for GKP Koktem, Aktau Waste Management Project non-technical summary, v4, 16.07.2012 (https://www.ebrd.com/english/pages/project/eia/41578.pdf): waste sorting + anaerobic digestion + biogas CHP, "a pilot project of the kind in Kazakhstan"; 15 000 t/yr digestate composting, digestate "mixed with another organic matter (e.g. garden waste, wood chips or bark)". No capacity, tariff or construction status in the document.
- **Green waste in MSW (Astana/Nur-Sultan)** — Abylkhani et al. (Nazarbayev University), ESPR 2020, https://doi.org/10.1007/s11356-020-08431-x (open manuscript https://strathprints.strath.ac.uk/73510/): organics 46.3 % of MSW, of which "large compostables (… and green waste, 13.3%)"; the sorting plant separates organics but sends them to landfill; municipal-service waste peaks in April–June with "year-long yard residues".
- **City practice** — Kursiv, 05.10.2026 (https://kz.kursiv.media/2026-10-05/devy-chem-vredit-almaty-voina-akimata-s-osennei-listvoi/): Almaty: collected leaves are "вывозится для утилизации на городской полигон". The Ministry of Ecology, as cited in the same article, says leaves go to composting. 5region.kz, 02.09.2026 (https://5region.kz/zhech-nelzya-a-kuda-devat-akimat-almatinskoj-oblasti-vpervye-podrobno-otvetil-na-voprosy-ob-utilizacii-travy-i-vetok): Almaty oblast "отдельной системы приема растительных отходов пока нет". The 2019 draft Almaty rules required leaves to go to "компостирование, производство биогаза…" (https://www.zakon.kz/politika/4989352-v-almaty-planiruyut-izmenit-pravila.html). Astana's landfill "утилизацией отходов … не занимается" (https://tengrinews.kz/kazakhstan_news/okolo-40-eyfelevyih-bashen-skolko-musora-nakopilos-v-astane-556319/).

Bayazitova): thermophilic laboratory digestion of food waste to make fertiliser, not green waste (https://shokan.edu.kz/en/departments/departament-nauki-i-kommercializacii-tehnologii/realizaciya-nauchnyh-proektov/3-irn-ap09259015-razrabotka-tehnologii-effektivnoj-pererabotki-organicheskih-othodov-metodom-termofilnogo-sbrazhivaniya-s-polucheniem-biologicheskogo-udobreniya-bayazitova-zulfiya-erzatovna/). ## Simulators and modeling tools

Simulators found: no new candidate. Hermes correctly named one tool: Any Logic, used in Temirbekova et al. 2020 (the DOAJ abstract confirms it). AnyLogic is general-purpose commercial simulation software, and the authors' C++ model is not released, so it was not added as a digester-simulator candidate.
