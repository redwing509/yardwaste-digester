## Summary

No Polish plant was found that digests mainly or only yard/garden waste today. The first green-waste-majority municipal digester is planned in Rybnik: about 22,500 Mg/yr of biodegradable waste, "z czego 60 proc. stanowić będą odpady zielone z ogródków, parków i innych terenów zielonych", dry horizontal continuous fermentation, about 100 mln zł, grant/loan signed with NFOŚiGW in Oct 2025, construction not yet started. Operating municipal digesters already take garden waste mixed with kitchen waste: ZUOK "Orli Staw" (near Kalisz; kitchen + garden biowaste "trafiają do fermentera", 1,400 m³ fermenter, ~21 days, 100–120 m³ biogas per tonne) and Suchy Las near Poznań (~42,000 t/yr, 7 dry-fermentation chambers, green waste also composted with the digestate; how much green waste is digested: NOT FOUND); Poland has 10 municipal biowaste digesters. The Olsztyn plant (30,000 t/yr, wet two-stage, up to 1 MW, start-up from May 2026) is mainly for kitchen waste because wet fermentation needs wet feed. Polish yard-waste yields are lab data: road-verge grass from Białystok 274.18 ± 22.59 NL CH4/kg VS (spring, fresh, wet fermentation) down to 182.63 ± 0.48 (summer silage, dry fermentation). Poland generated 2,157 thousand Mg of green waste in 2022 (≈15.5 % of MSW).
