## Summary

China has a large lab literature on digesting garden waste (leaves, grass, weeds, branches), mostly as co-digestion with food waste or sludge and with pretreatment, but no Chinese full-scale plant that digests garden waste was found in open sources (NOT FOUND). A 2013 national estimate put the energy potential of Chinese garden-waste biomass at about 260 PJ (all conversion routes, not digestion only). Lab methane yields for mixed garden + kitchen + fruit/vegetable waste rose from 396 to 601 mL CH4/g VS with ultrasonic pretreatment (Tsinghua, 2020). Grass alone gave 490.75 mL biogas but only 285.06 mL methane per g VS after 28 days (Tianjin, 2022). A 2025 South China Agricultural University study found that using digester liquid (biogas slurry) instead of water for hydrothermal pretreatment raised green-waste methane potential by 62.40%. Zhejiang's food-waste standard (DB33/T 1180-2019) says digester biogas "should" exceed 50% methane, and mentions garden waste only for co-composting.

## Verified findings

- **National potential — Shi et al. 2013** (Y. Shi, Y. Ge, J. Chang, H. Shao, Y. Tang, Renew. Sustain. Energy Rev. 22:432–437, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rser.2013.02.003; abstract https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/rensus/v22y2013icp432-437.html): garden-waste biomass biofuel potential "estimated at 260 petajoules (PJ), accounting for 20.7% of China's urban residential electricity consumption, or 12.6% of China's transport gasoline demand in 2008"; green space grew 12.7% a year from 1996 to 2008.
- **Pretreated garden + kitchen + fruit/veg co-digestion — Xu, Jiang, Meng & Yan 2020** (Tsinghua School of Environment; 环境工程 38(11):168–174, https://doi.org/10.13205/j.hjgc.202011028, open abstract http://hjgc.ic-mag.com/cn/article/doi/10.13205/j.hjgc.202011028): methane yield 396 mL CH4/g VS untreated → 601 (ultrasound) and 536 (alkali-heat); microwave slightly lower; CH4 in biogas >50% after day 2, peak 61.4–63.8% on days 11–12.
- **Weeds, eucalyptus and canna leaves with food waste — Tang, Wan, Zhang & Luo 2012** (环境工程学报, open abstract https://www.hjgcjsxb.org.cn/cn/article/id/8514): methane 827.0 (mixed weeds), 307.9 (small-leaf eucalyptus) and 287.3 (canna) mL/g VS degraded; freeze-thaw pretreatment +25.0% (weeds) and +85.4% (canna). (Hermes attached these numbers to a different 2014 paper.)
- **Compost pretreatment — Sun, Wan & Luo 2014** (环境工程 2014, https://doi.org/10.13205/j.hjgc.201403024): 0–9 days of aerobic composting before digestion; 7 days gave the fastest start and highest gas and methane content (no yields in the open abstract).
- **Grass, AD then hydrothermal carbonisation — Wang, Zhu, Song & Li 2022** (Tianjin University; 化工学报 73(2):904–913, https://doi.org/10.11949/0438-1157.20211287, open PDF on the journal site): cumulative biogas after 7/14/21/28 d = 174.62/368.74/449.32/490.75 mL/g VS; cumulative methane = 72.87/240.52/254.06/285.06 mL/g VS; max CH4 content 57.50%; theoretical 415.90 mL CH4/g VS. Best hydrochar after 7 d AD: mass yield 62.75%, HHV 23.81 MJ/kg, energy yield 80.31%. Companion paper (Wang et al., Sci. Total Environ. 807:151014, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scitotenv.2021.151014): leaves, branches, grass; best energy recovery 90.2% for leaves after 21 d AD (78.2% hydrochar + 12.0% CH4). (Hermes reported "495.74 mL CH4/g VS" for grass; that number was not found and the real methane figure is 285.06.)
- **Fallen poplar leaves — Wang & Wang 2009** (Tsinghua; 中国环境科学 29(2):181–185, open PDF https://www.sciengine.com/doi/pdf/68FAC2A9061D4C81A998DDDA432BE026): alkali-heat hydrolysis at 170 °C, 0.04 g NaOH/g TS → 344 mL biogas/g in 20 days, +90% vs untreated; semi-dry digestion of the hydrolysate at 2.65 g/(L·d) gave a 25.3% methane conversion (carbon basis). (Hermes gave an unrelated URL and a DOI that does not resolve.)
- **Grass cuttings + sewage sludge — Xie, Zhu, Du, Hu & Wang 2015** (Open Fuels & Energy Sci. J. 8:77, CC BY, https://doi.org/10.2174/1876973x01508010077): 50% grass (dry weight) raised gas production to 348 L/kg TS, +30% vs sludge alone. This is total gas, not methane. Institution NOT FOUND in open metadata.
- **Greenhouse crop residues (not garden waste) — Luo et al. 2014** (农业部规划设计研究院, Beijing; 农业工程学报 30(15):256–263, https://doi.org/10.3969/j.issn.1002-6819.2014.15.033): watermelon vine 508.85 mL biogas/g TS (highest of five; grass lowest); two-phase leachate-spray mode 53–56% CH4.
- **Biogas-slurry recycling — Zou et al. 2025** (South China Agricultural University, Guangzhou, team of Xie Jun and Yu Qiang; Chem. Eng. J. 505, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cej.2025.159204; university news https://www.scau.edu.cn/2025/0107/c17648a397429/page.htm): calls green waste China's "second-largest urban organic solid waste"; ensiling with sterilised slurry +10.17% (3 months) and +14.65% (6 months) methane potential; slurry as hydrothermal pretreatment medium +62.40% (green waste) and +47.46% (ensiled green waste).
- **Acidification step** — Yang, Jiang, Xu & Liu 2022 (Tsinghua; 环境工程 40(5):103–108, https://doi.org/10.13205/j.hjgc.202205015): fruit/veg + garden waste at 45 °C, pH 6 → VFA 12.94 g/L on day 11 (3.37× uncontrolled pH). Hu, Zhang & Wang 2024 (Zhengzhou; 水处理技术 2024(11), http://www.qikanzj.com/hek/scljs/mulu/488753.html): thermophilic sludge + garden waste with 8% CaO2 → max VFA 214.8 mg/g VS, but CaO2 inhibited methanation.
- **Plants**: no full-scale Chinese plant digesting garden waste found (NOT FOUND). The Suzhou Industrial Park "food-waste and garden-waste" project (phase 1, 2018; EPC by Purac, http://cn.purac.com.cn/content/details27_363.html) lists 300 t/d food waste + 100 t/d press liquor, 30,000 m3 biogas/day upgraded into the city gas grid; garden waste is not listed as digester feed.
- **Rules**: DB33/T 1180-2019 (Zhejiang engineering standard for food-waste utilisation, in force 1 Mar 2020; text https://www.cnww1985.com/news/show-1641.aspx): "厌氧消化产生的沼气甲烷浓度宜大于50%" (should be >50%); garden waste appears only as a co-composting material. GB 12348 (cited by Hermes as a methane rule) is the industrial-boundary noise emission standard. No national rule specific to garden-waste digestion was found.
