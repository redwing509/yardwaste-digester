## Summary

Switzerland collects garden and green waste separately and treats it in composting and digestion plants. Roughly half of it is digested. In 2013, the federal environment office (BAFU) counted 368 plants that processed 1,255,844 t of biogenic waste. About 50 % was composted, 11 % went to agricultural co-digestion and 39 % to 27 industrial digesters. Garden and landscape-maintenance waste made up 349,000 t, or about 19 % of all material including manure. Canton Zurich is further along. In 2025 its plants handled 244,261 t, about 70 % of it digested (64.6 % in 9 industrial digesters), and fed 51 GWh of biogas into the gas grid. Landscape maintenance supplied 37 % of the input. Swiss plants usually digest green waste mixed with household food waste or manure. Wood and branches are taken out and composted or burned. Named plants are Biogas Zürich (Werdhölzli), Courtemelon JU (a farm digester using about 5,000 t of green waste plus manure), Ecorecyclage Lavigny VD, Axpo Kompogas Uzwil SG and the PôleBio plant now under construction at Satigny GE. No Swiss plant digesting only yard waste was found, and no Swiss measured grass or leaf biogas yield was found.

Shared amine-scrubber upgrading with the sewage-sludge digesters; site biomethane 53,580 MWh. VSG 2023 brochure (https://gazenergie.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/e-paper/GE-Biogas/GE-Biogasanlagen-2023-de.pdf): "Jahreskapazität: 30'000 t Biomasse", "Energieproduktion: 65 GWh/Jahr", "Grüngut und Klärschlamm" — **65 GWh is the whole site including sewage gas**; the digester "ersetzte das offene Kompostierwerk, wo bis 2013 Gartenabfälle zu Kompost verarbeitet wurden."
- **Courtemelon JU (EcoBioVal Sàrl)** — operator (https://ecobioval.ch): "la partie fermentescible (gazons, déchets de jardin,…) des déchets verts sont introduits dans l'installation de biogaz. - **Support** — SBV 2024 factsheet: by the energy law the local network operator "doit acheter le biogaz proposé"; ASIG biogas fund pays investment and 36-month injection contributions; "certains cantons soutiennent les projets d'injection". Federal feed-in tariff plants (BFE open data, https://data.geo.admin.ch/ch.bfe.biogasanlagen/biogasanlagen/biogasanlagen_2056.csv.zip): 153 plants (133 agricultural, 20 industrial, including Kompogas sites); no feedstock field.
