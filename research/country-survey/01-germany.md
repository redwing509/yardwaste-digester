## Verification (checker)

Germany has a documented commercial and municipal practice of dry / high-solids anaerobic digestion of garden, park and biowaste, plus a 2025 DBFZ pilot biorefinery in Leipzig. Most separately collected green waste is still composted. Several headline numbers in the Hermes draft were misread or attached to the wrong year or plant stage.

## Corrections to Hermes (summary)

1. The first (4.2 min, PARTIAL) draft was too thin and was not published; this note is based on the r2 draft after a source check.
2. 21.9 is million tonnes of fresh matter per year of mobilizable potential, not 21.9 m³ CH4/t TS.
3. 170–332 L CH4/kg mixed two different series: untreated literature is 270–332; hydrothermally pretreated Pilot-SBG tests are 170–189.
4. The DBFZ educt data sheet is openly available; it was not paywalled.
5. Langenau input is 15,000 t/a; the “1,000 Nm³/t FM” yield was invented.
6. The Bioenergy International Enger article is dated 30 January 2018, not 2022.
7. AWM Munich’s current official figures (2012 folder) are 25,000 t/a capacity and 2,600,000 kWh in 2011, with expansion in 2007–2008, not 9,500 t/a, 930,000 kWh, 400 households and a 2006 expansion.
8. “232 / 24 plants,” “10.4 TWh,” the EUBCE 2015 yield triplet, ATB 2000 point values, and several 2025–2026 reports were not confirmed and were not used.
9. The simulator follow-up mis-attributed STRIKE-GOLDD to DBFZ (it is Villaverde et al., GNU GPL v3, https://github.com/afvillaverde/strike-goldd/); it also under-specified BENSIM’s license (CC BY 4.0 on Zenodo) and called LIBPF German-origin (simevo, Italy).

## Simulators and modeling tools

Simulator follow-up job `20261114-213924-digester-survey-01-germany-simulators-fo` (key `dg-survey-01-germany-sims`, rc 0, 9:39:32–9:47:53 PM America/Los_Angeles 14 Nov 2026) was citation-checked. Nothing was installed. Installing any of the tools below still needs a separate OK.

**Simulators found**

- **SIMBA#biogas** (ifak Magdeburg / ifak technology + service GmbH). Commercial dynamic simulator for wet-digestion biogas plants (ADM1, multi-substrate influent, reactors, gas handling, control). Official product page: https://www.ifak.eu/en/products/simba-biogas . License is proprietary / commercial (the page links to pricelists; exact prices were not taken). The downloads section lists the current version as SIMBA# 7.0; the same page body also describes a 4.2 release, so a single current-version number was not taken from the draft. A bibliographic record for use at the 25th SIMBA-Anwendertreffen (Magdeburg, May 2018; Weinrich and Pröter, FH Münster OPUS docId 16448) appears in a search; the OPUS page itself timed out (HTTP 504) this pass. Hermes’ “2023 lecture” wording was not used.

- **BENSIM (BioENergy SImulation Model)** (Markus Millinger, Helmholtz-Centre for Environmental Research, UFZ). A system-level, myopic recursive model of competition among bioenergy technology options in Germany (least-cost mix, endogenous learning, GHG accounting). It is not a plant-level anaerobic-digestion simulator. Official description: https://www.ufz.de/index.php?de=41629 . Public release 3.0 on Zenodo, 14 May 2019, license **CC BY 4.0**: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.2810903 . Hermes’ “academic / free for research (no commercial licence)” wording was not kept; the verified license is CC BY 4.0. “Developed with DBFZ support” was also not kept: the official description names UFZ / Millinger. DBFZ is a partner on the related BEniVer project page (https://www.ufz.de/index.php?de=46330).

- **BENOPT (BioENergy OPTimisation model)** (UFZ; Millinger and later co-workers). Official page: https://www.ufz.de/index.php?en=37180 . A perfect-foresight optimization model for allocating bioenergy across transport, power, heat and selected material sectors (BENOPT-DE / BENOPT-EU). Found while checking BENSIM; it was not a named find in the Hermes follow-up. The BEniVer page now lists BENOPT as the UFZ model. Zenodo version 2.1, 2 October 2020: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4063962 . Zenodo states the license as “Other (Open)”; the exact SPDX name was not confirmed from the LICENSE file without unpacking the archive.

- **STRIKE-GOLDD** (Alejandro F. Villaverde and co-workers). A MATLAB toolbox for structural identifiability and observability of nonlinear models. **Not a DBFZ-developed toolbox.** Official repository: https://github.com/afvillaverde/strike-goldd/ , license **GNU GPL v3**. DBFZ researchers (Hellmann, Hempel, Streif and Weinrich, arXiv:2301.05068, 5 April 2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.05068) used it on ADM1-R3 and ADM1-R4 models. Hermes listed DBFZ as the developer, called the license only “free for academic use,” and said no permanent URL was found.

**Not added as Germany collection candidates**

- “BioSim”: the MathWorks user story (https://www.mathworks.com/company/user_stories/transforming-anaerobic-digestion-research-through-simulation.html) confirms a DBFZ junior research group reimplemented ADM1-R3 models in MATLAB / SimBiology. It does not name a public tool called BioSim or give a download URL.
- A UFZ double-membrane gasholder transient model: mentioned as a publication, with no standalone software release found.
