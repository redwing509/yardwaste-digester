## Summary

Qatar has one documented digester line for green waste: Module A of the Domestic Solid Waste Management Centre (DSWMC) at Mesaieed, run by Keppel Seghers under a 20-year design-build-operate contract (operating since 2011). A 2012 seminar report says three of its fifteen digesters take green waste, but that "green waste" includes market and catering/kitchen waste as well as yard waste. Woody oversize is burned, not digested. In 2012 the green-waste line was fed about 36 tonnes a day. Newer government reports (2025–2026) talk about compost and electricity from the centre, but they do not say green waste is still digested. A 2025 plan to grow the centre to 5,300 t/day frames green waste as feed for compost. Qatar's AD research (Qatar University) models food waste, manure and sewage sludge, not yard waste. Landscaping green waste is composted, for example at Hamad International Airport. No Qatari methane yield for grass, leaves or palm fronds, no green-waste tonnage and no biogas or biomethane tariff was found.

## Verified findings

- **DSWMC Mesaieed, green-waste digesters (2012 description)** — Gulf Times report of a QGBC seminar (February 2012): "The pre-treatment has two streams; green waste (yard, biogenous, market, and catering/kitchen waste) and Municipal Solid Waste (MSW). The treatment comprises five modules of three anaerobic digesters each." "While three digesters of Module A are reserved for green waste processing, the balance (Modules B to E) uses MSW." "The green waste is shredded and sieved before small pieces are fed into the digester for a 14-day process. The big pieces are incinerated." "Currently, average feeding is at 36 tonnes daily." https://www.gulf-times.com/story/32905/worlds-largest-composting-plant-in-mesaieed
- **DSWMC plant figures (operator)** — "The anaerobic digestion and composting plant not only handles 750 tonnes of waste daily but also generates up to 109,000m³ of biogas, translating into 6.8MW of green electricity and heating." The site handles 2,300 t/day, of which "800 tonnes are either recycled or composted, while the remaining 1,500 tonnes are converted into energy." These are plant-wide figures that include MSW, not a yard-waste yield. https://www.keppelseghersbelgium.com/qatar-dswmc
- **DSWMC feed split in a Qatar University study** — Ghanimeh et al., Earth Systems and Environment 9(3):1923–1936 (2025, open access): "the DSWMC recycling center in Doha processes up to 2300 mt/d of waste, of which 800 mt are to be sent daily to the anaerobic digester and 1000 mt to incineration" (citing QDB 2022). The same paper models AD of OFMSW, livestock manure and sewage sludge only ("850 million m³ of biogas and 2.5 million metric tons of digestate"); yard waste is not one of its streams. https://doi.org/10.1007/s41748-025-00698-9
- **DSWMC today (state agency and ministry)** — QNA, 1 June 2025: the centre "receives 2,200 tonnes of per day" and "also disposes of dead animals, green waste, and slaughterhouse wastes"; it makes "green and organiccompost, up to 40 tons/day" and "compost from domestic waste, up to 170 tons/day" (as printed). https://qna.org.qa/en/news/news-details?date=1%2F06%2F2025&id=domestic-solid-waste-management-centre-dswmc-in-mesaieed-clean-environment-and-sustainable-circular-economy — Ministry of Municipality via Al Raya (18 Nov 2026): 250,000 MWh of electricity and 32,000 t of organic compost from the centre in 2025. https://www.raya.com/2026/11/18/وزارة-البلدية-تحويل-نفايات-مسيعيد-إلى-كهرباء-وسماد-عضوي
- **Planned expansion (2025)** — Al Arab, 1 July 2025 (Arabic), quoting the ministry's waste treatment director: capacity to rise "بمقدار 3 آلاف طن يوميا، ليصل إجمالي الإنتاج إلى 5300 طن يوميا" (by 3,000 t/day to 5,300 t/day), and the plan adds new waste types "مع التركيز على النفايات المنزلية، والمخلفات الخضراء وتحويلها إلى سماد" (focusing on household waste and green waste and turning them into compost). https://alarab.qa/pdf/20250701_1751323078-787.pdf
- **Qatar University PhaseMix reactor (lab)** — plug-flow digester with local mixing for "organic waste" (Ghanimeh, Al Momani): "successfully demonstrated at laboratory scale"; "Provisional Patent Application Filed"; "TRL 4–5". - **Biogas for vehicles (2017)** — the Ministry of Municipality and Environment and Qatar University's College of Engineering "launched a project to produce biogas from wastes" for vehicles; "An experimental study of the project was conducted by powering a vehicle with biogas produced from waste". Feedstock not stated. https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/22/12/2017/Project-to-explore-biogas-fuel-launched
- **Landscaping green waste is composted** — Hamad International Airport composts "green waste generated during landscaping activities" and expects "about 3,979 tonnes of organic compost on an annual basis". https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/23/04/2022/qatars-airport-to-recycle-waste-for-landscaping-and-provide-compost-to-third-parties

## Not taken (unverified, contradicted or not yard waste)

- The 109,000 m³/day biogas, 6.8 MW and "129 tonnes of biogas" figures are plant-wide (MSW included), not yard-waste yields.
- The 800 t/day figure appears twice with different meanings: "recycled or composted" (operator page) and "sent daily to the anaerobic digester" (2025 paper citing QDB 2022). Neither is a green-waste figure.
- QNA's "48.8 megawatts/day" is printed with a unit that is not a power unit; it is not used.
- Al Meezan legal portal (Arabic law text): blocked, so no article is quoted.
- HBKU portable-drum composting thesis (2026): aerobic composting with dried leaves and wood chips, not AD.

NOT FOUND: a Qatari methane or biogas yield for grass, leaves, prunings or palm fronds; green-waste tonnage for Qatar; whether Module A still digests green waste in 2025–2026; dry AD, membrane upgrading, AnMBR, HTC or torrefaction on green waste; biogas or biomethane tariffs or grid-injection rules; any QAR cost figure for a green-waste digester.
