## Summary

No Australian plant was found that digests mainly or only garden organics (GO). Councils route GO to composting or gasification: Logan City Council's ARENA-funded FOGO study planned AD for 10,000 wet t/yr of commercial food organics and gasification for up to 30,000 t/yr of GO, because UQ bench tests found GO hard to digest. The one Australian yard-waste methane number found is a lab BMP from that UQ work: "The 100% GO sample, which was a blend of grass, leaves and branch clippings, had the lowest BMP at 150 mL CH4/g VS" (food organics 400; 50/50 FO/GO 200; "Higher GO fractions are not justified"). Operating Australian digesters treat food, industrial or abattoir waste (Richgro 2 MW, >35,000 t/yr; Goulburn abattoir). Projects that may take FOGO: Horsley Park (120,000 tpa, "potential to accept" FOGO), EMRC Red Hill (WA; "will also consider using dry anaerobic digestion" for FOGO), and NALG's Midcoast plant (NSW; Kompogas dry AD + tunnel composting, 146,000 t/yr incl. food organics). A Cowra trial (2017 approval) planned to co-digest "green trash and Municipal Green Waste"; results NOT FOUND.

## Verified findings

- **Logan City Council FOGO feasibility (QLD) — study, AD of FO + gasification of GO** — Lessons Learnt Report, Rev 2, 9 Oct 2024 (https://arena.gov.au/assets/2024/10/Logan-City-Council-FOGO-Feasibility-Study-Lessons-Learnt-Report.pdf): "the GO was predominately woody waste"; UQ BMP: "The highest BMP was recorded for 100% FO, with a value of 400 mL CH4/g VS. The 100% GO sample, which was a blend of grass, leaves and branch clippings, had the lowest BMP at 150 mL CH4/g VS. WAS had a BMP of 200 mL CH4/g VS"; "BMP of 200 mL CH4/g VS for a 50% FO/50% GO sample"; "Higher GO fractions are not justified"; "Samples of 100% GO were modelled, and the results showed that GO was not a highly digestible material, and as such, this would require extremely large digesters"; financials: "payback of over 30-years for both scenarios". Public Study Report, Rev 1, Aug 2024 (https://arena.gov.au/assets/2024/10/Logan-City-Council-FOGO-Feasibility-Study-Final-Public-Study-Report.pdf): "Stage 4 … 10,000 wet tonnes of commercial FO via AD and 30,000 tonnes of GO via gasification"; "GO are harder to digest but have a high calorific value. These are therefore better suited for gasification after drying".
- **Richgro, Jandakot (WA) — operating, food waste** — CEFC case study, last updated May 2016 (https://www.cefc.com.au/case-studies/richgro-green-thumb-grows-green-power/): "The 2 MW anaerobic digestion plant can process more than 35,000 tonnes of commercial and industrial organic waste each year"; feed "solid and liquid commercial food waste"; $6.6 m plant, $2.2 m CEFC loan; digestate used in Richgro garden products. No garden organics in the feed.
- **Goulburn Bioenergy (NSW) — completed 2018, abattoir waste** — ARENA (https://arena.gov.au/projects/goulburn-bioenergy-project/): "built an anaerobic digester that captures biogas from the breakdown of effluent and organic waste from the Southern Meats abattoir"; 18 May 2017–23 Oct 2018; "capacity to displace 75% of peak load". No tonnage stated.
- **Horsley Park Bioenergy (NSW) — funded 2026–2029** — ARENA (https://arena.gov.au/projects/horsley-park-bioenergy-project/): "approximately 120,000 tpa of organic waste into renewable biomethane"; "circa 253,161 GJ" a year piped to the Austral Bricks kiln; feed commercial/industrial/agricultural "with potential to accept separated food organics (FO) and/or food organics garden organics (FOGO)". ARENA $10.5m of $68.34m.
- **Scenic Rim Agricultural Industrial Precinct / Kalfresh (QLD) — under construction, farm waste** — Coordinator-General (https://www.coordinatorgeneral.qld.gov.au/projects/find-a-project/completed-projects/scenic-rim-agricultural-industrial-precinct): "Approved with conditions", $291 million, "1.6 megawatt (MW) (scalable to 10 MW) renewable energy power facility", "50,000 tonne per annum composting facility". ABC News, 19 Feb 2026 (https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-02-19/qld-kalfresh-bioenergy-scenic-rim/106363982): "processing offcuts, farm waste and rotational crops into renewable natural gas"; "at full capacity … up to 31,000 homes or … 98 million kilometres"; construction began 19 Feb 2026; "$80 million investment from capital funding and the QIC". Qld statement (https://statements.qld.gov.au/statements/104553): investment from QIC and Wollemi Capital; "agricultural and food waste". Not municipal yard waste.
- **EMRC Red Hill (WA) — composting now, dry AD considered** — Waste Authority case study, Oct 2021 (https://wasteauthority.wa.gov.au/images/resources/files/2022/06/21220392-2_FOGO_case_studies_update_EMRC.pdf): interim FOGO composting "up to 10,000 tonnes"; "The permanent facility will also consider using dry anaerobic digestion technology to process the FOGO material"; resource recovery facility "up to 150,000 tonnes of waste per annum" to "produce compost and biogas".
- **Midcoast Regional Organic Processing Solution (NSW) — planned, dry AD** — Waste Management Review, Nov 2025 (https://wastemanagementreview.com.au/organics-to-energy-revolution-nalgs-hybrid-solution-tackles-fogo-pfas/): NALG "integrates Kanadevia Inova's advanced dry anaerobic digestion Kompogas technology with NALG's proprietary in-vessel tunnel composting technology"; MC ROPS "poised to handle 146,000 tonnes of organic waste annually, including food organics and commercial food by-products". GO share of digester feed: NOT FOUND. (Checker find.)
- **Cowra co-digestion trial (NSW) — approved 2017** — CLEAN Cowra, Mar 2017 (http://clean.org.au/blog/2017/3/13/development-application-for-the-aggregated-biomass-co-digestion-trial-approved): at the Cowra WWTP; "the feedstocks will be primarily "wet and semi- dry" (green trash and Municipal Green Waste)"; "about 6- 7 feedstocks"; "possible use of a Covered Anaerobic Lagoon". Trial results: NOT FOUND.
- **Regulation (QLD)** — Energy from Waste Guideline, effective 2 Dec 2021 (https://www.qld.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0028/834661/waste-strategy-guideline-energy-from-waste.pdf): defines AD as "the biological breakdown of organic matter by microorganisms and enzymes, in the absence of oxygen to produce biogas and digestate"; its AD case study is wet digestion of sewage biosolids (Urban Utilities). No yard-waste plant.
