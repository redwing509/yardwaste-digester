## Summary

In Israel green waste is chipped, not digested. Israeli rules push it there: "it is illegal to landfill unchipped green waste, and burning green waste is prohibited". Hiriya Recycling Park (Dan Region) "handles approximately 400 tons of green and bulky waste daily", shredding branches and trunks into wood chips for compost, sludge stabilisation, ground cover and fuel. Israel's best-known digester, the ArrowBio plant at the Tel Aviv transfer station, is a "70,000 ton per year anaerobic digestion plant" for mixed MSW using water separation and a UASB (2003, reported 2005); no green-waste feed is described. No Israeli yard-waste digester, BMP or simulator was found.

Using an ArrowBio module, the facility has two components: A front-end physical element for separation and preparation purposes; and a back-end biological system with an Upflow Anaerobic Sludge Blanket (UASB) digestion"; feedstocks are "tipped into vats of circulating water".
