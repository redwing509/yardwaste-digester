## Summary

India's organic-waste digestion is built around source-segregated wet municipal waste, cattle dung and crop residue, not yard waste. Under Swachh Bharat Mission-Urban 2.0, the government told the Rajya Sabha on 20 July 2026 that 143 MSW-based compressed biogas (CBG) plants with 21,760 TPD capacity are "at various stages of implementation", while 11 CBG plants (1,085 TPD) and 18 bio-methanation plants (2,150 TPD) are operating now. The largest operating MSW CBG plant is Indore (EverEnviro): 550 t/day of segregated organic waste, about 15 t/day CBG. The only Indian measurements for yard waste are laboratory tests. Leaf and yard waste from the IISc Bangalore campus gave 160–250 mL biogas/g VS over 100 days at 40–50 % methane; at IIT Kharagpur, electrochemical pretreatment raised yard-waste biogas from 287 to 446 mL/g VS. Parks and leaf litter are mostly composted. No Indian plant digesting mainly yard or garden waste was found.

At present, 11 Compressed Bio Gas (CBG) plants with designed capacity of 1,085 TPD and 18 Bio-methanation plants with designed capacity of 2,150 TPD are operational."; "17 Waste to Electricity plants with designed capacity of 20,050 TPD are operational"; SBM-U 2.0 approved "Bio-methanation plants of 9173.72 TPD" and WtE 5128.74 TPD, "total project cost of ₹2683.63 Cr having central share of ₹803.27 Cr"; SWM Rules 2026 (from 1 April 2026) "mandate four-stream segregation of waste at source".
- **National scale (Dec 2025)** — Rajya Sabha Unstarred Question 1662, answered 15/12/2025 (https://sansad.in/getFile/annex/269/AU1662_HXnkd9.pdf?source=pqars): "1,62,468 ton per day (TPD) of Municipal Solid Waste is generated … Out of which 1,30,484 TPD is processed"; budget 2023-24: "500 new "Waste to Wealth" plants under the Galvanizing Organic Bio-Agro Resources Dhan (GOBARdhan)"; "145 Compressed Bio Gas (CBG) plants … with cumulative capacity of 20,155 TPD under GOBARdhan scheme"; "17 Bio-methanation plants with designed capacity of 1,910 TPD are operational".
- **Indore CBG (EverEnviro / Indore Clean Energy Pvt. Ltd.)** — EPR Magazine, 3 Jun 2024 (https://www.eprmagazine.com/special-report/everenviro-compressed-biogas-plant-sets-a-global-standard-in-sustainable-innovation/): "India's largest MSW-based CBG facility"; "handles 550 MT of organic waste daily. It can produce 17 tonnes of BioCNG and 40 MT of organic fertiliser daily"; "consistent production rate of nearly 15 tonnes per day (TPD) of compressed biogas (CBG)", peak 18 t (March 2024); 30–40 days digestion; methane 96 % after upgrading, compressed to 250 bar. Feedstock is source-segregated wet MSW (plastics and metal removed); no garden-waste share stated.
- **Pune, Katraj Gaon (PMC + Mailhem Engineers)** — Rao, Parthan & Doshi, IWMI/Routledge case (https://cgspace.cgiar.org/bitstreams/d1f6a157-0ddc-44f8-ad58-2f84b35df964/download): "Biogas plant operational since 2009"; "Output: 300–325 m3/day processing 5 tons/day and electricity generation of 144 MWh/year, 180 tons/year of bio-sludge used manure"; "The bio-sludge is used as a manure in the 112 municipal parks and gardens maintained by PMC". Input is the organic fraction of ward MSW; garden waste is listed among "Solid waste other than MSW" collected separately.
- **Market and city plants (PIB)** — PIB backgrounder, 27 Sep 2026 (https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2315501&lang=2&reg=48): Bowenpally, Hyderabad: "roughly 10 tons of vegetable and fruit waste are collected every day … It generates around 400–500 units of electricity and 30 kg of biofuel a day". Lucknow, PIB 21 Jan 2026 (https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2216806&lang=1&reg=3): "LMC and Bhumi Green Energy have set up three waste processing plants of 700 MT/day capacity each … Organic waste is processed into compost and biogas" (no biogas figure).
- **Yard-waste yield, IISc Bangalore** — Ravi Kumar, Chanakya, Bhatia & Dasappa, CRC Press 2021 (https://doi.org/10.1201/9781003204435-12, abstract): "Leaf litter and yard waste comprise nearly 35% of the solid wastes in such places"; lignin 24 % VS; "Approximately 160–250 mL biogas/gVS of biogas was produced for 100 days under biochemical methane potential conditions, with a methane concentration of 40–50%"; "neither the inoculum levels nor the particle sizes play a significant role".
- **Yard-waste pretreatment, IIT Kharagpur** — Panigrahi & Dubey, Bioresource Technology 2019 (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biortech.2019.121958, abstract): electrochemical pretreatment with graphite electrodes; "the ultimate biogas production of 446 mL/g VS was achieved in comparison to the untreated yard waste of 287 mL/g VS on 35th day"; "net energy gain of 4.75 kJ/g VS" and "net profit of 518 rupees (US$ 7.4) per 1 ton of yard waste".
- **Older IISc leaf-biomass digesters (titles only)** — Jagadish, Chanakya, Rajabapaiah & Anand, "Plug flow digestors for biogas generation from leaf biomass", Biomass and Bioenergy 14:415–423, 1998 (https://doi.org/10.1016/s0961-9534(98)00003-8); Chanakya, Bhogle & Arun, "Field experience with leaf litter-based biogas plants", Energy for Sustainable Development 9:49–62, 2005 (https://doi.org/10.1016/s0973-0826(08)60492-5).
