## Summary

Belgrade's park waste is composted or burned for heat, not digested. JKP "Zelenilo Beograd" maintains "oko 3.000 hektara javnih zelenih površina", collects "oko 8.000 metara kubnih" of leaves a year and takes leaves, branches and mown grass to its Biobaza, where large branches are used "за огрев, односно, користе се за грејање стакленика" (as fuel to heat greenhouses) and leaves are composted (news, Nov 2022). Serbia has "44 biogasna postrojenja koja rade u kapacitetu od oko 44 MW" (Biznis.rs, Jul 2025); none was found that takes yard waste. The only Serbian green-waste AD number found is a literature value in a 2023 review: "369 m3" of methane per tonne of organic dry matter from green waste.
