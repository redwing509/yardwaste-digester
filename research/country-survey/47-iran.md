## Summary

No Iranian digester taking yard or garden waste was found. Iran's only operating biogas power plant named in the opened sources is at the South Tehran wastewater treatment plant and runs on sewage sludge ("تنها نیروگاه بیوگاز ناشی از تصفیه فاضلاب در ایران", ILNA 22 Aug 2020). The one Iranian-team experiment with yard waste is a 2022 batch study at Imam Ali University (Tehran): campus green waste ("cuts of green space on the campus and fallen leaves") co-digested with food waste gave the most biogas at 50:50 and a total methane yield of 425 mL/g VS. A 2020 University of Canterbury (New Zealand) conference abstract digested yard waste collected in a Tehran park and got at most 115.42 L CH4/kg VS added under thermophilic-wet conditions. Other Iranian AD studies opened (OFMSW, fruit and vegetable waste, sewage sludge with algae, HTC pretreatment, ADM1 modelling) do not use yard waste.

## Verified findings

- **Imam Ali University food + green waste batch study** — Ahmadi-Pirlou et al., Journal of Environmental Science Studies 7(3), autumn 1401 (2022), 5202–5215, doi 10.22034/jess.2022.336809.1762 (https://www.jess.ir/article_152302.html, open PDF, CC BY 4.0): green waste from the campus; "The highest biogas production was determined in the ratio of FW and GW 50:50. About 90% of methane yield was obtained after 21 days of digestion with a total yield of 425 ml/g VS." Methane yield rose from 245.8 to 425 mL/g VS across the mixing ratios. High-solids AD (15–25 % TS) gave 36.7, 27.3 and 37.9 % more volumetric methane than liquid AD at 5 % TS; 25 % TS gave the lowest methane. Caution: the abstract closely follows the structure and wording of a Chinese study (Chen et al., Bioresource Technology 154 (2014) 215–221, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24398149/) but with different numbers.
- **Tehran park yard waste (non-Iranian team)** — Bayat, Bello-Mendoza & Wareham, University of Canterbury, ICBWTP 2020 conference abstract (https://hdl.handle.net/10092/101653; https://publications.waset.org/abstracts/111217/…): "Twenty liters of GY waste was collected from a public park located in the northern district in Tehran"; "A maximum methane yield of 115.42 (L methane/ kg VS added) was obtained for the GY thermophilic-wet AD combination", 240 % above mesophilic-dry; VS reduction 53 %, sCOD reduction 84 %.
- **South Tehran sewage biogas plant** — Tehran Times (https://www.tehrantimes.com/news/451553/), Trend (https://www.trend.az/business/energy/3288010.html) and ILNA (Persian, 01/06/1399 = 22 Aug 2020): SATBA signed a 15-year guaranteed purchase "از شهریور سال ۱۳۹۸ تا شهریور سال ۱۴۱۳" (Aug/Sep 2019 to Aug/Sep 2034) at a base price of 10,500 rials/kWh, under Article 62 of the law on government financial regulations; the plant is at "Tehran's southern sewage filtration center"; quoted output "44 gigawatts" of electricity per year and 140 TJ heat, enough for a town of 60,000 people. The "gigawatts per year" unit and the "500 billion kWh over 15 years" total are inconsistent in the sources and should not be used as numbers.
- **Iranian AD research on other substrates (not yard waste)** — University of Tehran HTC study, Environmental Energy and Economic Research 6(1) 2022 (https://www.eeer.ir/article_140757.html): hydrochar at 150 and 190 °C raised biomethane from Tehran OFMSW by 35.88 % and 47.33 %, and hydrochar-230 cut it by 29 %. Bu-Ali Sina University, Environmental Engineering Research 28(5) 2023 (https://www.eeer.org/journal/view.php?number=1459): sewage sludge + Cladophora algae, "The highest amount of biogas (235.17–296.03 ml/g VS)". Salehiyoun et al. 2019 (https://doi.org/10.1007/s10163-019-00887-5): replacing OFMSW with fruit/vegetable waste raised methane concentration "up to 68%". Sabour et al. 1392 SH (2013) ADM1 abstract (civilica 223653, search snapshot): simulated Tehran OFMSW "around 0.49 Nm3/d per m3 … methane fraction was 53%".
- **Review** — Masjoudi, Shayegan & Babaee (Sharif University), Iranian Chemical Engineering Journal 18(104), 2019 (https://www.ijche.ir/article_107578.html): AD "یک فرایند مناسب برای دفع پسماندهای شهری در ایران تلقی می‌گردد" (is considered a suitable process for municipal waste in Iran); recommends thermal pretreatment with alkali and two- or multi-stage reactors.

## Simulators and modeling tools

Simulators found (after check): none Iranian, none added. Design-Expert (Stat-Ease, commercial, https://www.statease.com/software/design-expert/) was really used by the Bu-Ali Sina sludge/algae study, but it is statistical design-of-experiments software, not a digester simulator. A 1392 SH conference paper solved its own fruit/vegetable-waste mass and energy balances in MATLAB (commercial; model not released). ADM1 (IWA) is a model, not GPL software; ADM1 implementations are already on the candidate list. ASPEN Plus and an "Excel experimental design" were not in the cited papers and were dropped.

## Not taken (unverified or contradicted)

- "420 L methane/kg VS, 70–95 % conversion" as an Iranian (Azad Quchan) result — it is the abstract of Bouallagui et al. 2005 (Tunisia), Process Biochemistry 40: 989–995, reproduced in an Iranian review; fruit/vegetable waste.
- "Tehran University collaboration" for the Tehran-park study — the authors are at the University of Canterbury, New Zealand.
- "29 ml/g VS" (University of Isfahan thesis) — a theoretical chemical potential with an ambiguous Persian decimal ("29/692"); fruit/vegetable waste.
- "44 GW nominal capacity", "pilot-scale plants … 44 GW", "~60 k households", plant feedstock "municipal waste, green waste, food waste" — unit error, 60,000 people, and a generic biogas definition; the plant runs on sewage sludge.
- University placed in "Ardabil province" (Imam Ali University is in Tehran; only the inoculum came from Ardabil province).
- Years: Sharif review 2019 (not 2020), HTC paper 2022 (not 2020), sludge/algae paper 2023 (not 2022).
- Simulator rows: ADM1 "GPL", "Custom MATLAB model (Sabour et al.)", ASPEN Plus, "WASET experimental design (Excel)".

NOT FOUND: an Iranian digester taking mainly yard/garden waste; a measured Iranian BMP for yard waste alone; Iranian digestate data from yard-waste AD; an Iranian law or incentive aimed at yard-waste digestion; an Iranian-developed released digester simulator.
