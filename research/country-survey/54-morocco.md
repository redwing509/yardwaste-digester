## Summary

No Moroccan digester running on yard, park or street green waste was found. The closest items are a design study and one small lab test. A 2023 Mohammed V University master plan for the Mohammadia landfill sizes a dry batch digestion and composting unit that adds 15 % green waste to the organic fraction and uses green waste as composting structurant (design only, not built). A 2026 lab test in Rabat put green waste second of four Moroccan wastes for biogas (189 mL cumulative in 40 days, no replicates, low methane). A Rabat AD consultancy (Generizon) recommends digestion for food and household organics but composting for green waste ("there is really no need to landfill tree cuts"). Measured Moroccan lignocellulosic AD work is on oasis date-palm residues (farm, not urban) at Université Ibn Tofail, Kenitra: 133 NmL CH₄/g VS for palm leaflets in a lab CSTR (2021), and an AD + digestate-combustion route giving 1.39 MWh for palm leaflets vs 1.17 MWh by combustion alone (2023). Older regional studies size manure-dominated biogas potential, and note that of more than 350 small digesters built since the early 1980s about 45 % were not working properly.

## Verified findings

- **Green waste → compost; AD for food/household organics (company view)** — Generizon Sarl, Rabat (https://generizon.com/wte-integrated-approach-organic-waste-to-energy-morocco-africa/): "Considering the organic-rich composition of Moroccan MSW, generizon positions anaerobic digestion (AD) as the more technically adapted and context-relevant WtE pathway". Its scheme: CSTR AD of source-separated organics, plug-flow AD of OFMSW with RDF from dried digestate, and "Composting of green waste, as there is really no need to landfill tree cuts". Company page, not a plant.
- **Mohammadia landfill master plan (design)** — Farhat A., Aziz A., Lagliti K., Fekhaoui M. (Mohammed V University, Rabat), Nature Environment and Pollution Technology 22(3) (2023) 1469–1479, doi 10.46488/NEPT.2023.v22i03.032 (https://neptjournal.com/upload-images/(32)D-1465.pdf). Mohammadia household waste 54.94 % organic. Dry batch mesophilic digestion (BEKON garage process, 37 °C, about four weeks), 980 m³ digesters; 15 % green waste added to the organic fraction; green waste = 40 % of digestate volume in composting tunnels. Result as printed: "9 digesters to be built, 6.700 MW.y-1 of electrical energy produced, 14.523 tons.y-1 of refined compost, and 2.128.680 m3.y-1 of biomethane" (point used as thousands separator: 2 128 680 m³/yr; 6.700 MW·y⁻¹ number format ambiguous).
- **Green waste lab test (biogas)** — Kouchane Y., Agoud S. (Mohammed V University, Rabat), Alsayer I., Mabrouki J. (Northern Border University, Saudi Arabia), Bahadur P.S. (Amity University, India), Frontiers in Physics 14 (2026) 1875880, doi 10.3389/fphy.2026.1875880 (https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physics/articles/10.3389/fphy.2026.1875880/full). Moroccan household waste, green waste, poultry droppings, tannery waste; 40 days at 35 °C; cumulative biogas 266 / 189 / 160 / 150 mL (poultry / green / tannery / household); no replicates; methane low; urban vs farm not stated.
- **Date-palm leaflet AD (lab CSTR)** — Kerrou O., Lahboubi N., Bakraoui M., Karouach F., El Gnaoui Y., Schüch A., Stinner W., El Bari H. (Université Ibn Tofail, Kenitra; University of Rostock; DBFZ Leipzig), J. Material Cycles and Waste Management 23 (2021) 1599–1608, doi 10.1007/s10163-021-01238-z. Mesophilic semi-batch CSTR: 133 NmL CH₄/g VS, biodegradability 33 %, OLR 2.76 kg VS/m³·d; modified Gompertz R² = 0.998.
- **Date-palm leaflet pretreatment** — Kerrou O., Lahboubi N., Bakraoui M., El Bari H. (all Université Ibn Tofail, Kenitra), "Improving methane production from palm leaflets waste with thermal and ultrasound pre-treatment", Physics and Chemistry of the Earth A/B/C 132 (2023) 103482, doi 10.1016/j.pce.2023.103482. Moroccan team confirmed; abstract and results not opened (paywalled), so no numbers are taken.
- **Date-palm AD + digestate combustion** — Lahboubi N., Kerrou O., El Bari H. (Université Ibn Tofail) with Sarh B., Boushaki T., Belandria V., El Khattabi Y. (ICARE-CNRS, Orléans), Clean Energy 7(4) (2023) 747–754, doi 10.1093/ce/zkad020. Date palm waste "from a Moroccan oasis" (farm residue): digestate LHV 12.93 MJ/kg (empty fruit bunch) and 12.71 MJ/kg (palm leaflet); combustion alone 1.13 / 1.17 MWh vs AD + digestate combustion 1.27 / 1.39 MWh.
- **Regional biogas potential (manure-dominated)** — Beraich F.Z., Arouch M. (Université Hassan 1er, Settat), Bakasse M. (Université Chouaib Doukkali, El Jadida), IJERT 3(11), Nov 2014 (https://www.ijert.org/research/the-development-of-anaerobic-digestion-technology-and-the-potential-of-biogas-in-moroccan-regions-of-doukkala-abda-chaouia-ourdigha-tadla-azilal-IJERTV3IS110063.pdf): Doukkala-Abda "about 5, 6 million tons per year" → "more than 0,14 'Mtep'"; total 0,46 Mtep/yr for three regions; >350 digesters since the early 1980s, 10–180 m³, about 45 % not working properly; a 20 m³ dome digester on a Khouribga farm runs a gas engine for water pumps. Afilal M.E., Belakhdar N. (Université Mohammed Ier, Oujda), Bakx A., Membrez Y. (EREP SA, Switzerland), J. Renewable Energies 13(2), doi 10.54966/jreen.v13i2.193: Oriental 4.8 Mt/yr (78 % manure) → "0,090 Mtep"; Al Hoceima-Taza-Taounate 7.8 Mt → 0,130 Mtep; Tanger-Tétouan 6.7 Mt → 0,110 Mtep.
- **Olive-mill waste HTC (not AD)** — Dahdouh A. et al. (International University of Rabat; CNRS / Université de Lorraine), Biomass and Bioenergy 184 (2024) 107205, doi 10.1016/j.biombioe.2024.107205: recycling the HTC aqueous phase raised hydrochar yield from 47.4 to 53.0 wt % and energy yield from 72.8 to 81.3 %. Agro-industrial residue.
