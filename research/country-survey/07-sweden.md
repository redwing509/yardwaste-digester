## Summary

Sweden is one of the few countries where garden waste goes **into** the digester, not only into composting. Swedish source-separated food waste is wet, nitrogen-rich and low in structure, so the dry plug-flow plants built since 2013 mix in shredded or chipped garden waste to get the right structure and carbon balance and to dilute ammonia. Västblekinge Miljö's Mörrum plant (2013, Scandinavia's first dry-AD plant) was built for 15,000 t food waste plus 5,000 t garden waste per year; Gästrike Ekogas in Forsbacka, Gävle (2017) screens and chips garden waste and mixes it with food waste in a thermophilic plug-flow digester. Across four Nordic dry-AD plants, Energiforsk measured 400–610 Nm3 CH4 per tonne VS (mixed food waste with some garden waste). No Swedish plant digesting garden waste alone was found. Roadside grass is the most-studied pure green feedstock: Trafikverket harvested verge grass for Gasum's Jordberga plant (2020–2022), and a 2025 IVL/Bio+ study found drying and pelleting raised roadside-grass methane output by about 24% per g VS.

## Verified findings

- **Västblekinge Miljö AB (VMAB), Mörrum** (Process Nordic 7/2015, https://www.processnet.se/article/view/438101/torrotningen_sprider_sig_fran_morrum; Energiforsk 2019:609). Owned by Karlshamn, Sölvesborg and Olofström municipalities; SEK 54 M, completed 2013. Capacity 15,000 t/yr food waste + 5,000 t/yr garden waste (design 20,000 t/yr; ~13,000 t food waste treated in 2017–18). Thermophilic 55 °C, mean retention 27 days, feed ~500 kg/h, ~10% rejects (mostly plastic). Gas = 2.6 million litres petrol-equivalent per year (2015 figure) or about 14–15 GWh vehicle gas (Energiforsk). Food waste and garden waste pass a grinder and star sieve into two parallel plug-flow digesters. In 2015 the operator also mixed garden waste into the digestate to raise dry matter for separation; ~15,000 t/yr digestate was planned to be split into liquid biofertiliser and compost. The plant moved from thermophilic to mesophilic operation in Oct–Nov 2017 for process stability.
- **Gästrike Ekogas, Forsbacka (Gävle)** (Gästrike återvinnare, https://gastrikeatervinnare.se/atervinning/vad-hander-med-ditt-avfall/; Energiforsk 2019:609). In operation since 2017; 17,000 t/yr capacity (Energiforsk), 54 °C, Thöni plug-flow; digestate SPCR 120 certified. Garden waste is screened, chipped and used in biogas production: "a suitable mix of food and garden waste makes the digestion process work efficiently". An operator-sponsored local-news feature (marketing) describes mixing in twigs and leaves to control carbon content, 52 °C, ~30 days, 30,000 t/yr.
- **Energiforsk 2019:609, "Dry anaerobic digestion of food waste at mesophilic and thermophilic temperature"** (Persson, Westerholm, Schnürer, Nordin, Tamm, Nordberg; IVL, SLU, Linköping University, RISE; https://energiforsk.se/media/26807/dry-anaerobic-digestion-of-food-waste-energiforskrapport-2019-609.pdf). Four dry plug-flow plants: HEMAB Härnösand (2017, 6,000 t/yr), VMAB Mörrum, Gästrike Ekogas, and Labio in Lahti (**Finland**). Specific methane 400–610 Nm3 CH4/t VS fed (food waste typically ~500); volumetric 2.36–4.98 Nm3/m3·d. Mixing shredded yard waste into Swedish food waste is common practice for structure; park and garden waste also dilute ammonia. Operating at 42–48 °C may still meet animal-by-product hygiene rules at high ammonia, and 42 °C needed 16–25% less heat than 39 °C or 52 °C.
- **Trafikverket roadside-grass pilot, Skåne** (Bioenergy International, 15 Jun 2020, https://bioenergyinternational.com/swedish-transport-administration-to-pilot-verge-grass-collection-for-biogas; results as cited in IVL C10127). Small state roads in Trelleborg, Skurup and Svedala, three seasons from 2020; a special mower collects cuttings for Gasum's Jordberga plant (which takes ~90,000 t/yr green waste and farm/food-industry residues for 110 GWh/yr biomethane and 80,000 t biofertiliser). Expected >700 t CO2 saved over three years. Follow-up (Emilsson et al. 2024, cited in the IVL report): 0.3–1.2 t CO2e saved per hectare of verge per harvest; energy-positive at 0.5–1.75 t DM/ha; litter was no problem at the plant and heavy metals no obstacle, but high phosphorus limits digestate spreading.
- **IVL / Bio+ "Biogasproduktion från pelleterad halm, vägkantsgräs och vass"** (IVL report C10127, Aug 2025, ISBN 978-91-7883-742-7, open PDF https://bioplusportalen.se/app/uploads/2025/11/2023-01056_Hjort_Slutrapport.pdf; project page https://www.ivl.se/english/ivl/our-offer/research-projects/energy/biogas-production-from-straw-grass-and-reed.html). IVL with Halmstad University, Södra Hallands Kraft, Biofrigas, Perstorp, Volvo Technology; Dec 2023–Mar 2025; SEK 1.6 M (Swedish Energy Agency). Roadside grass from Blekinge (~3.5 t, ~70% water) was co-pelleted with wheat straw. In 35-day BMP tests, dried and pelleted reed and roadside grass gave on average 50% and 24% more methane per g VS than untreated; pelleted straw showed no significant gain. Pelleting is very costly if drying is needed; co-pelleting wet grass into dry straw avoids drying. National potential: roadside grass and reed ~0.3–1 TWh/yr (roadside grass alone 0.5–1.5 TWh), straw 2–4 TWh/yr.
- **Lund University student thesis** (Sara Waxegård, 2013, https://lup.lub.lu.se/student-papers/record/3558903). For Lund's garden-waste plan: the soft, non-woody fraction (grass, fallen fruit, flowers) suits biogas best, European plants use dry fermentation, and residents can sort garden waste into two fractions. It states Sweden then had no experience digesting garden waste (the same year Mörrum opened).
- **National context**: Swedish biogas production was 2,395 GWh in 2024 (+6%), 68% upgraded (Energimyndigheten, 2025, https://www.energimyndigheten.se/nyhetsarkiv/2025/biogasproduktionen-okade-i-sverige-under-2024/).

## Policy

- **Separate biowaste collection**: from 1 Jan 2024 municipalities must collect food and kitchen waste close to properties (Regeringen press release, 22 Dec 2022, https://www.regeringen.se/pressmeddelanden/2022/12/forbattrad-insamling-av-matavfall-kan-ge-mer-biogas); ~400,000 t/yr more food waste could be handled. The EU biowaste definition includes garden and park waste, but no Swedish mandate for separate garden-waste collection was found.
- **Klimatklivet** (Naturvårdsverket investment grant, since 2015; official status reports seen as search snapshots): on average 48% of investment cost; biogas production is the largest category (~SEK 5.5–5.8 bn granted). No specific dry-AD rate was confirmed.
- **Digestate certification**: Avfall Sverige's "Certifierad återvinning" (SPCR 120) is a **voluntary** quality label (https://www.avfallsverige.se/fakta-statistik/avfallsbehandling/biologisk-atervinning); nearly all Swedish biofertiliser goes to farmland.
