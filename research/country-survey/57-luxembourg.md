## Summary

Luxembourg does digest part of its green waste, but only the soft fraction. Minett-Kompost's dry plug-flow digester in Mondercange (Strabag, started 2011) takes about 25,000 t a year of kitchen waste and grass clippings from the green/brown bin and makes about 2.3 million m³ of biogas, upgraded to about 1.25 million m³ of biomethane for the SUDGAZ grid. Tree and shrub cuttings (the woody yard waste) skip the digester and go to composting or wood chips for heating. Since April 2026 the City of Luxembourg's brown-bin biowaste, garden waste and grass included, also goes to Mondercange. The farm co-operative plant Naturgas Kielen (Kehlen, since 2010, about 50,000 t/yr, wet process at about 40 °C) mainly digests manure, slurry and silage with municipal biowaste. Its 2008 permit also lists 1,251 t/yr of plant waste from gardening firms. Research is at LIST (anaerobic-digestion microbiology, bioaugmentation, a 1–300 L bioreactor platform) and at the University of Luxembourg (a 2015 PhD on grass and maize silage). No Luxembourg study gives a methane yield for municipal yard waste.

## Verified findings

- **Mondercange (Minett-Kompost) — what gets digested** — Garaffa (Novamont) and Gröll (IGLux, Luxembourg), BioCycle 54(10), 25 Oct 2013 (https://www.biocycle.net/anaerobic-digestion-of-compostable-bags/): 21 municipalities (185,000 residents); AD added in 2011 to a 20,000 tpy composting plant; 30,000 tpy plant capacity, digester 25,000 tpy; "The green waste stream for AD includes grass clippings, weeds, etc. Other yard waste — primarily tree and shrub cuttings with a high proportion of wood — goes directly to the composting operation."; up to 4,000 tpy energy-crop silage in winter; aerobic hydrolysis 2–3 days, then mesophilic 106 °F (about 41 °C), 25 % TS, HRT 21 days in a Strabag LARAN plug-flow reactor; digestate pressed to 40 % TS and tunnel-composted.
- **Mondercange — output** — Minett-Kompost 25-year brochure (PDF 2019; https://www.dippach.lu/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Brochure-Minettkompost.pdf): "jährlich aus ca. 25.000 t Küchenabfällen und Grasschnitt aus der Biotonnensammlung ca. 2,3 Mio. Kubikmeter Biogas"; amine scrubbing to ca. 1,25 Mio. m³ biomethane into the SUDGAZ grid (up to 1.000 households, up to 2.500 t CO₂/yr saved); 1.900 m³ horizontal fermenter, 3-week plug flow; 34.000 t organic waste/yr from 21 communes → 9.000 t compost; woody green-waste fraction → 10.000 m³ wood chips since 2016 for biomass heating. European Commission project page (seen only as a search-index copy; page returned errors): design up to 2 800 000 Nm³ biogas = 1 500 000–1 700 000 Nm³ purified gas; EUR 23.4 million, of which EUR 4 million ERDF (2007–2013).
- **City of Luxembourg brown bin (2026)** — Chronicle.lu, 22 Apr 2026 (https://chronicle.lu/category/at-home/60795-vdl-updates-biowaste-collection-sorting-rules): the city joined Minett-Kompost; "Kitchen organic waste and grass contribute to biogas production, while other garden waste acts as structural material that helps ensure suitable processing conditions." Garden waste from larger works is "shredded and processed into compost or wood chips for heating".
- **Naturgas Kielen, Kehlen (farm co-op co-digestion)** — Ministry of the Environment permit, 29 Oct 2008 (https://environnement.public.lu/dam-assets/documents/emweltprozeduren/etablissements_classes/eid/arretes-ied/structure-arretes/5-3-b-i_002_naturgaskielen_kehlen/NaturgasKielen-07CF04-29102008.pdf): yearly 17'530 Mg slurry, 12'737 Mg manure, 4'500 Mg maize silage, 3'412 Mg green-forage silage, 1'000 Mg plant waste from members, 1'251 Mg plant waste from gardening firms, 7'691 Mg separately collected biowaste (SICA), 300 Mg supermarket waste → about 4'561'056 m³ biogas. Bioenergie-promotion.fr, 5 Nov 2010 (https://www.bioenergie-promotion.fr/8986/naturgas-kielen-le-premier-biogaz-produit-au-luxembourg/): 30 farmers, running since July 2010, 50.000 t/yr, grass clippings among inputs, digesters at 40 °C, biogas ±55 % CH₄ upgraded to >98 % by pressurised water scrubbing; called the largest biogas plant in Luxembourg (2010 claim).
- **National waste figures** — STATEC, Luxembourg in Figures 2025 (https://statistiques.public.lu/dam-assets/catalogue-publications/luxembourg-en-chiffres/2025/luxembourg-in-figures-2025.pdf): 2022 municipal waste without inert waste 346.8 thousand t, of which household 301.2; composted 21.7 %, recycled 29.8 %, incinerated with energy recovery 42.0 %; 461 kg household waste per person.
- **Research (LIST)** — LIST, 20.10.2026 (https://www.list.lu/media-events/news/from-manure-to-methane-how-microbes-turn-waste-into-green-energy): digesters usually at about 37 °C; digestate solids roughly 4–15 %; LIST showed digestate instead of chemical fertiliser reduces nitrogen pollution risk (feedstocks: manure, crop leftovers, food waste — not yard waste). LIST "Microbiome Designer" (https://researchportal.list.lu/projects/detail/microbiome-designer): Cloacimonetes-enriched bioaugmentation against acidosis. LIST Greentech Innovation Centre, 25.07.2023 (https://www.list.lu/media-events/news/news-detail/list-launches-new-facilities-in-its-greentech-innovation-centre): 1–300 L bioreactor platform, EUR 2 million. FNR, 28.03.2019 (https://www.fnr.lu/research-with-impact-fnr-highlight/spotlight-nature-does-it-best/): LIST group uses termite-gut microbes for lignocellulose pretreatment.
- **Research (University of Luxembourg)** — Benito Martín P.C., PhD 2015, Engineering Research Unit (https://orbilu.uni.lu/handle/10993/21823): mesophilic AD of lignocellulosic substrates under overloading, "Optimisation and modelling"; full text is open on ORBilu (not opened by the checker). Related records are on grass and maize silage (farm energy crops, not yard waste).
- **Policy** — Loi du 21 mars 2012 (https://legilux.public.lu/eli/etat/leg/loi/2012/03/21/n1/jo), Art. 2(1)(e): straw and other natural non-hazardous agricultural or forestry material used on the farm or for energy is excluded from the waste law. Règlement grand-ducal du 31 juillet 2024 (summary: https://www.klima-agence.lu/fr/nouvelles-subventions-pour-le-biogaz-au-luxembourg): biomethane tariffs for first injection from 1 Jan 2023 of 133 EUR/MWh (≤150 Nm³ raw biogas/h) or 90 EUR/MWh, 70 EUR/MWh after 15 years, plus a 30 EUR/MWh slurry bonus. Nothing specific to green waste.
