## Summary

Canada co-digests yard waste with food waste in high-solids (dry) digesters; no Canadian plant digesting only or mainly yard waste was found. The best-documented case is Edmonton's High Solids Anaerobic Digestion Facility (40,000 t/yr organics design + 8,000 t wood-chip bulking agent), whose feed "Primarily … includes municipal solid waste, SSO from institutional waste sectors, and yard waste"; its final report gives real operating numbers: "The HSADF processed 32,767 tonnes of feedstock in 2022 and 31,548 tonnes in 2021 … producing more than 2.6 million m3 of biogas in 2021 containing about 47% methane" (checker arithmetic: about 82 m³ biogas, about 39 m³ CH₄ per tonne of feed; not a source figure). Harvest Power's Richmond (BC) demonstration was "Canada’s first ever high solids anaerobic digester", designed for "27,000 tonnes per annum (tpa), consisting of food and yard waste". Québec biomethanation plants mostly refuse yard waste: SÉMECS says grass "produit très peu de biogaz" and leaves and branches "ne produisent pas suffisamment de biogaz"; RECYC-QUÉBEC lists green waste (grass and leaves) with biogas potential "Non déterminé", "Biodégradabilité : Faible", "Prétraitement requis : Important". One Québec exception in RECYC-QUÉBEC's April 2026 directory: CTBM (Saint-Pie) biomethanation lists "résidus verts (résidus de feuilles, gazon, arbres)" among accepted inputs (non-exhaustive; throughput NOT FOUND).

## Verified findings

- **Edmonton High Solids AD Facility (AB) — operating, food + yard waste, dry/high-solids** — ERA project page (https://www.eralberta.ca/projects/details/implementation-of-high-solids-anaerobic-digestion-technology-at-the-edmonton-waste-management-centre/): "to turn food and yard waste into renewable energy and compost"; "breaks down organic waste, like food scraps and yard trimmings, without oxygen"; "designed to process 40,000 tonnes of residential and commercial organic waste into biogas and compost per year"; project value $41.7 M, ERA $10 M; "completed in 2020"; the city "is looking into more cost-effective options for organics processing" (121,000 t/yr city-wide organics expected by 2027). Final Outcomes Public Report 2023 (https://www.eralberta.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/City-of-Edmonton-Waste-Management-Final-Report.pdf): "seasonal spikes in yard waste"; "8,000 tonnes of wood chips as a bulking agent for a total material handling capacity of 48,000 tonnes"; 2021: 31,548 t → ">2.6 million m3 of biogas … about 47% methane and 37% CO2"; 2022: 32,767 t; "For the first four months of 2021, most of the biogas produced was flared"; design 20,000 t compost, 12.5 million kWh electricity, up to 42,100 GJ heat. Maple Reinders (https://www.maple.ca/projects/edmonton-high-solids-anaerobic-digestion-facility/): "Size 48,000 tonnes/year Completed 2018"; feed "source-separated organics and mechanically separated organic fraction of municipal solid waste, including yard waste". Alberta Major Projects (https://majorprojects.alberta.ca/details/High-Solids-Anaerobic-Digestion-Facility/3004): schedule 2018–2021, $42.9M.
- **Harvest Power, Richmond (BC) — demonstration, food + yard waste, two-stage batch HSAD** — NRCan Clean Energy Fund (https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-sources/clean-fuels/urban-waste-electricity-demonstration?wbdisable=true): CEF $5 M of $22.5 M; "it has not previously been used in Canada to treat a mixed organics stream of yard and food waste"; "designed for an input capacity of 27,000 tonnes per annum (tpa), consisting of food and yard waste"; "consistently process feedstock rates at or above the design rate"; about 14-day process; expected 17,687 tpa compost and 6,789 MWh/yr net electricity from a 1 MW CHP (design values). The 65–80 % CH₄ on the page is a generic two-stage figure "Depending on feedstock".
- **Convertus, East Gwillimbury (ON) — approved, SSO + IC&I (no yard waste stated)** — ERO 019-7624 (https://ero.ontario.ca/notice/019-7624): "200 tonnes of the liquid Organic Waste per day, 1,800 tonnes of the solid Organic Waste per day, and 200,000 tonnes … per year"; RNG up to 20,000,000 Nm³/yr; SSO "consisting mainly of: food waste/kitchen scraps soiled paper diapers pet waste". Decision posted 30 Oct 2024.
- **Northwood / RIC (EOR), Oshawa (ON) — approved, SSO + IC&I (no yard waste stated)** — ERO 019-1538 (https://ero.ontario.ca/notice/019-1538): "up-to a maximum of 200,000 tonnes of the organic waste per year"; biogas "approximately 25,064,765 normalized cubic metres … per year"; "a maximum of 32,613 tonnes of waste shall be stored in Phase 1". Decision posted 1 Nov 2021.
- **GrowTEC, Coaldale (AB) — operating, farm-scale, manure + food waste** — ERA (https://www.eralberta.ca/projects/details/source-separated-organics-and-agricultural-waste-anaerobic-digester/): "manure, spoiled potatoes and food scraps"; $7.23 M project, ERA $3.57 M; completed 2018; "over 4000 megawatt-hours of electricity". No yard waste.
- **Ontario context** — Pulp & Paper Canada, 11 Jul 2025, Sarah Stadnyk (Canadian Biogas Association) (https://www.pulpandpapercanada.com/the-untapped-potential-of-biogas-in-solving-ontarios-organic-waste-challenge/): "According to Ontario’s Food and Organic Waste Framework , 32% of the province’s waste stream consists of food and other organic materials such as food scraps, soiled paper, and yard waste"; "Ontario currently hosts 140 biogas facilities … more than 1 million tonnes".
- **Québec (FR sources)** — SÉMECS (https://www.semecs.ca/gazon-feuilles-branches/): grass "contient beaucoup de fibres végétales (cellulose et lignine)", "termine ultimement sa course à l’enfouissement. Il produit très peu de biogaz"; leaves and branches "ne produisent pas suffisamment de biogaz". SÉMECS accepted/refused list (https://www.semecs.ca/procede-de-traitement/matieres-acceptees-et-refusees/): refuses "Tourbe et gazon", "Feuilles d’automne", "Branches d’arbre"; materials needing "plus de 20 jours" are refused. RECYC-QUÉBEC (https://www.recyc-quebec.gouv.qc.ca/entreprises-organismes/se-conformer/matieres-organiques/implanter-gestion/biomethanisation-sur-place): green waste (grass and leaves) "Potentiel de biogaz : Non déterminé … Biodégradabilité : Faible … Prétraitement requis : Important". RECYC-QUÉBEC co-digestion study, Nov 2009 (https://www.recyc-quebec.gouv.qc.ca/sites/default/files/documents/analyse-potentiel-codigestion-mo.pdf): green waste "n’est pas idéal au procédé de biométhanisation … peuvent perturber le processus de digestion". RECYC-QUÉBEC facility directory, Apr 2026 (https://www.recyc-quebec.gouv.qc.ca/sites/default/files/documents/liste-installations-traitement-mo.pdf): CTBM Saint-Pie (biométhanisation) accepts "résidus verts (résidus de feuilles, gazon, arbres)" among other inputs; Québec City's CBAQ takes "Résidus alimentaires résidentiels et ICI, boues municipales".
- **Small / northern AD (not yard waste)** — Polar Knowledge Canada + NRC (https://www.canada.ca/en/polar-knowledge/education/turning-waste-into-low-carbon-fuel.html): food-waste screw press + high-rate digester, six-month pilot at CHARS, Cambridge Bay. Balance BioGas, Whitehorse Star 8 Mar 2023 (https://www.whitehorsestar.com/News/bio-digester-converts-organic-waste-to-usable-gas): "a 227-litre unit designed to convert household organic waste into usable gas". Mitacs / Toronto Metropolitan University + CCI BioEnergy (https://www.mitacs.ca/our-projects/optimization-of-small-scale-anaerobic-digesters-for-on-site-organic-waste-processing/): small on-site digesters; CCI converts "municipal green bin organic compost into renewable biofuels".
