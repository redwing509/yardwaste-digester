## Summary

Belarus has no anaerobic digester for yard or garden waste. Its biogas sector (37 complexes, 53,682 MW at 31 July 2023) is two kinds of plant: 16 farm digesters (21,219 MW) on livestock waste, such as Rassvet (4,8 MW, Mogilev region), and 21 landfill-gas installations (32,463 MW) on closed municipal landfills, such as the Vitebsk, Orsha and Novopolotsk plants. Municipal yard waste is composted: in Minsk, the city greening enterprise Minskzelenstroy opened a composting station in autumn 2021 for fallen leaves, cut grass and wood waste (about 4 000 m³ a month, designed for 70 000 m³ a year), and the compost goes into planting soil. Belarusian laboratory work found tree leaves unusable as a sole digester feed because of very high volatile-acid formation; crop grasses (Sudan grass, reed canary grass) gave low lab biogas yields over 20 days.
