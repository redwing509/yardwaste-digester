## Summary

South Korea has a large and growing biogas sector, but it is built on **sewage sludge, food waste and livestock manure**; no Korean plant, pilot or company digesting yard/garden waste was found in open sources (NOT FOUND). In 2023 Korea had 112 biogas plants (66 single-feed, 46 integrated) with 70,927 t/day capacity, producing 383,321 thousand Nm3 of biogas a year at an average of about 60% methane (KEITI trend report, 2025). The Biogas Act (enacted end of 2022, in force from 31 Dec 2023) names sludge, food waste and manure as its target feedstocks and sets public-sector conversion targets of 50% by 2025 and 80% by 2045 (private sector 10% from 2026 to 80% by 2050). Korean garden-waste work found in open sources is composting, not digestion. The only Korean food-waste + fallen-leaf digestion study found is a 2013 conference paper whose results are not openly available. The nearest lignocellulosic data is a Hankyong National University BMP test on giant miscanthus (an energy grass, not garden waste): 0.154–0.241 Nm3 CH4/kg VS added, only 30.7–48.0% biodegradable.

## Verified findings

- **Biogas Act — Kim, Kim, Yang, Koo & Jung 2025** (Chonnam National University, Gwangju; J. Korean Soc. Environ. Eng. 47(10):714–723, https://doi.org/10.4491/KSEE.2025.47.10.714, open full text, CC BY-NC): the Act "enacted in 2022 and enforced since 2023" (body: in force from 31 Dec 2023) targets "하수 찌꺼기, 음식물류 폐기물, 가축분뇨 등 생물학적 기원의 유기성 폐자원"; public sector must convert 50% of these wastes to biogas by 2025 and 80% by 2045; private sector 10% from 2026, 80% by 2050; the enforcement decree caps food waste at 30% of input in integrated digestion. 2022: about 110 public and private biogas plants, about 3.7억 (370 million) Nm3 biogas a year, replacing the city-gas use of about 370,000 households. Example: Miryang plant (2023), 20 t/day food waste + 80 t/day livestock manure → about 2,700 kWh/day. The paper does not mention garden, leaf or wood waste.
- **Plant statistics — KEITI trend report, June 2025** ("바이오가스 연료 활용 기술개발사업 기획을 위한 동향조사", prepared by 특허법인 이오 for 한국환경산업기술원, open PDF https://keiti.re.kr/common/board/Download.do?bcIdx=39059&cbIdx=318&fileNo=1&streFileNm=15a6a74fb40e4b60894e3ad9ead23d6f.pdf): 2023 — 112 plants (66 single-feed, 46 integrated), main inputs sewage sludge, food waste and livestock manure; capacity 70,927 t/day; biogas 383,321 thousand Nm3/yr, of which 326,110 thousand Nm3 used for power, city gas etc.; average methane ~60% (about 95 plants at 60–70%); more than 80% of organic waste still goes to feed or compost.
- **Policy background — KSP 2024/25 consulting report on Uzbekistan** (Ministry of Economy and Finance / Korea Eximbank, https://www.ksp.go.kr/api/file/download/27715): Korea's own history — "2005년 음식물쓰레기 직매립 금지제가 시행되었다" (direct landfilling of food waste banned in 2005). (Hermes credited this report with a London Convention / green-waste sentence that is not in it.)
- **Food-waste digestion research (not garden waste)** — Kim, Shin & Oh 2008 (KAIST / Hanbat; J. Korea Soc. Waste Manag. 25(8):716–722, KCI https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART001307167): two-stage digestion of food-waste leachate, 297 mL CH4/g COD added at 18 h HRT (UASB); 66.7% COD removal and 62.5% biogas recovery at 3 days total HRT. Choi, Cho & Oh 2018 (Hanbat National University; 한국도시환경학회지 18(1):101–105, https://m.earticle.net/Article/A330688): food-waste solids, best methane 0.25 ± 0.01 m3/kg VS added at HRT 40 d; CH4 <60% before upgrading.
- **Food waste + fallen leaves** — 김문환, 박상현, 김민호, 오세은, "음식물쓰레기와 낙엽 혼합물을 이용한 혐기성소화 연구", 유기성자원학회 학술발표대회논문집, 2013 (record https://openalex.org/W2259195438; DBpia page now returns 410): the only Korean food-waste + leaf digestion study found. Results NOT FOUND (no open abstract).
- **Lignocellulosic grass BMP — Yoo, Kim & Yoon 2017** (Biogas Research Center, Hankyong National University, Anseong; Korean J. Environ. Agric. 36(1):29–35, https://doi.org/10.5338/kjea.2017.36.1.03): giant miscanthus (Miscanthus sacchariflorus, energy crop), theoretical 0.502 Nm3 CH4/kg VS added, BMP 0.154–0.241 Nm3 CH4/kg VS added (biodegradability 30.7–48.0%); NDF 86.88%, ADF 62.91%; pretreatment needed. A 2011 review by the same group (Korean J. Soil Sci. Fert. 44(5):903–915, https://doi.org/10.7745/kjssf.2011.44.5.903) says Korean digestion research has focused on manure, food waste and sludge, and work on other plant biomass is "매우 미흡" (very lacking).
- **Garden waste is composted** — Korea National Arboretum garden-waste composting study (J. Korea Academia-Industrial Coop. Soc. 25(12):510–517, 2024, https://doi.org/10.5762/kais.2024.12.25.12.510; title only). A 2015 SME-ministry R&D report turned turf clippings plus food-waste leachate into fertilizer (https://scienceon.kisti.re.kr/srch/selectPORSrchReport.do?cn=TRKO201600008753), not biogas.
- **Plants, pilots, companies**: no Korean full-scale or pilot digester treating garden/yard waste and no company with a documented yard-waste AD project found (NOT FOUND).
