## Summary

Greek green waste is composted, mainly at the Ano Liosia plant (EMA) in Western Attica, which is set up for "Green waste (pruned materials) : 40,000 tons/year" alongside 60,000 t/yr of source-separated organics; its 2021 "Green Factory" upgrade aims at "high-quality compost". No Greek full-scale digester for yard waste was found. The key Greek lab result is from Patras/NTUA (Antonopoulou, Vayenas, Lyberatos, 2020): NaOH-pretreated grass lawn waste reached "427.07 L CH4/kg Volatile Solids (VS), which was almost 25.7% higher than the BMP of the untreated GL", with positive net heat "only when using a more concentrated pretreated slurry (i.e., 20% organic loading), or when applying NaOH pretreatment at a lower chemical loading".
