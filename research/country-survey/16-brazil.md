## Summary

Brazil has no plant that digests mainly yard or pruning waste. Municipal pruning waste mostly goes to landfill or composting: São Paulo city generated 18,400 t of pruning waste in 2016, "quase que integralmente destinados a aterros", even though landfilling it has been banned by municipal law since 2008 and by the PNRS since 2010; the city's five Feiras & Jardins composting yards take up to 4,700 t/yr of pruning. The closest thing to a yard-waste digester is Rio de Janeiro's Comlurb biomethanisation unit (Methanum/UFMG technology): solid-state batch AD with leachate recirculation, about 3,000 t/yr of large-generator food waste plus shredded park pruning as the carbon and bulking source, about 70 Nm³ biogas per tonne, over 300 t/yr of compost, and a 35 kW genset that powers the process (C40, January 2024). Brazilian yard-waste yields come only from laboratory and small pilot work: KOH-pretreated garden pruning gave 620 L N biogas/kg VS (UNINOVE, 2019), and adding grass clippings to food waste in a 500 L pilot raised biogas from 17 to 86 L/d and methane from 43 % to 67 % (UEL, 2021). Desk studies (Uberaba campus, São Carlos) find AD of pruning waste technically possible but not economic on its own. The best-documented Brazilian AD stream is sugarcane vinasse (high-rate UASB and fluidised-bed reactors), which is a liquid effluent rather than a lignocellulosic solid.

## Verified findings

- **Rio de Janeiro biomethanisation unit (Comlurb + Methanum + UFMG)** — C40 case study, Jan 2024 (https://www.c40.org/pt/case-studies/rio-biomethanisation-unit/): "digestão anaeróbica em lote no estado sólido com um processo de recirculação de lixiviados"; "capacidade anual de processamento de cerca de 3,000 toneladas"; feed "resíduos alimentares de grandes geradores … juntamente com resíduos de poda de áreas verdes – uma fonte crucial de carbono e material de volume"; "rendimento de biogás de aproximadamente 70 Nm³ por tonelada de resíduo orgânico"; "mais de 300 toneladas de composto orgânico por ano"; "motogerador de 35 kW". Pruning share of the feed: NOT FOUND. (Checker find.)
- **São Paulo city pruning waste** — Reis, Viana, Storel Júnior & Günther, chapter "Métodos de valorização de resíduos de podas e aparas", USP (https://repositorio.usp.br/directbitstream/2ee7957c-4b21-4431-aa1e-dd97cb26db81/cap%2011%20M%C3%A9todos%20de%20valoriza%C3%A7%C3%A3o.pdf): "Em 2016, por exemplo, o município gerou um total de 18.400 t de resíduos de podas e aparas que foram quase que integralmente destinados a aterros"; landfill estimate for 2015–2019 "ultrapassa as 23.000 toneladas" per year; Feiras & Jardins Sustentáveis "cinco pátios de compostagem … até 15,6 mil t/ano, com processamento de até 4.700 t/ano de resíduos de podas e aparas"; landfilling "proibido desde 2008 pela Lei Municipal do PAMPA e, desde 2010, pela Lei Federal da PNRS"; PGIRS 2014 foresees "Centrais de Biodigestão (anaeróbia)".
- **KOH pretreatment of garden pruning (UNINOVE)** — Bastos et al., GeAS 8(3), 2019 (https://doi.org/10.5585/geas.v8i3.15780): KOH 5 %, BMP at 35 °C for 25 days (VDI 4630); "a segunda batelada teve a maior produção de biogás (620 L N biogas/SV -1 kg) e uma eficiência de 30% quando comparado com o substrato não pré-tratado"; black-liquor reuse "eficiência média de 20%".
- **Grass clippings + food waste, 500 L pilot (UEL Londrina)** — Borth, doctoral thesis, 2021 (https://repositorio.uel.br/items/0603c037-d450-4e38-a12b-db41af778c24): "o Reator B (RRU+RPG) apresentou em média 73% de remoção de STV, produção de 86 L d-1 de biogás e 67% de CH4" vs food-waste-only Reactor A "17 L d-1 e 43% de CH4", at OLR 0.24 kg VS/m³·d; "dificuldade na manutenção de um processo estável, sendo necessária a adição de alcalinizante". (The BMP figure 509 NmL / 159 NmL CH4/g VS is for food waste only.)
- **Recife pruning + sewage sludge (lab)** — Silva et al. (UFPR/UFRPE/UFPE), Rev. Agronegócio e Meio Ambiente 14(1), 2021 (https://doi.org/10.17765/2176-9168.2021v14n1e007840, abstract via Crossref): "a composição T8 (12,5% biomassa de poda, 37,5% lodo e 50% água) foi a que apresentou maiores produções de metano"; potential "40,2 MWh de energia elétrica … com renda bruta de cerca de 29,6 mil reais ao ano"; compost vs digestate "relação C/N de 9,7 e 18,6, respectivamente".
- **Uberaba campus desk study** — Passaglia, Coelho & Anater (USP), Biofix 7(2), 2022 (https://doi.org/10.5380/biofix.v7i2.86113) and the underlying USP monograph (2021): campus pruning 144.3 t/yr → "14.646,45Nm³/ano" biogas → "15,94 MWh/ano" (Atlas factor, not measured); electricity savings "no máximo 2,1%, 1,1%, 3,8% e 3,2%" for densification, biodigestion, gasification, pyrolysis; investment "não se viabilizam". The monograph also quotes Ferreira et al. (2018): national sweeping + pruning waste "pode chegar 20.887.745,50 t/ano", potential "3.655.355.462,50 Nm³/ano de biogás" (secondary estimate).
- **São Carlos scenario study (UFSCar TCC, 2025)** — Carvalho (https://repositorio.ufscar.br/items/9ad95880-c0ae-4eff-a122-941c904ec88e): food waste + thermally pretreated pruning "increased the specific biogas yield by approximately 159% compared with landfill disposal"; up to "8% of the total electricity demand of the municipality of São Carlos". Model, not a plant.
- **National biogas context** — CIBiogás, Panorama do Biogás no Brasil 2024 (https://abiogas.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/PANORAMA-DO-BIOGAS-2024.pdf): 1,633 plants registered (1,587 operating), 4.7 billion Nm³/yr; "RSU ou esgoto representa 60% da capacidade produtiva instalada do país" (mostly landfill gas and sewage — out of scope; no yard-waste category).

### Sugarcane vinasse AD — separate from yard waste

- **Pilot UASB (EESC-USP)** — Del Nery et al., Biomass & Bioenergy 119 (2018) (https://bv.fapesp.br/pt/publicacao/153985/hydraulic-and-organic-rates-applied-to-pilot-scale-uasb-reac): "pilot-scale upflow anaerobic sludge blanket (UASB) reactor (120 L)", 700 days; "The methane content in the biogas was 68.8 +/- 7.14%"; "methane productivity reached the highest value of 8.059 LCH4.L-1.d-1 at 32.27 kgCOD.m(-3).d(-1)".
- **Two-stage fluidised bed (UFSCar thesis, 2019)** — Ramos (https://repositorio.ufscar.br/items/443f3ba5-3c2c-4089-8e5c-3bffff07066a): "2.71 ± 0.26 L CH4.day-1.L-1 to 3.18 ± 0.29 L CH4.day-1.L-1"; single-stage scale-up "28 MW (2.5 MW per million tons of sugarcane)".
- **Review (CTBE/CNPEM + EESC-USP, 2015)** — Moraes, Zaiat & Bonomi, RSER 44 (https://repositorio.usp.br/bitstreams/15fedb32-7dcf-48e0-becc-950e3d0d1011): "the current near-absence of anaerobic digestion plants in sugarcane biorefineries"; São Martinho mill "5000 m3 up-flow anaerobic sludge blanket (UASB) operated in thermophilic conditions … constructed in the 1990s"; fertirrigation "the most traditional destination given to vinasse since the late 1970s".
- **Lab reactors, biopolymer pretreatment (UFTM/UNAERP)** — Rocha et al., Ciência e Natura 44 (2022) (https://periodicos.ufsm.br/cienciaenatura/article/download/68825/46854?inline=1): "COD removal efficiencies from R1 and R2 were 82.9 ± 4.4% and 72.2 ± 18.1%"; pretreatment gave no improvement.
- **Reactor risk comparison (EESC-USP + Silesian Univ. of Technology)** — Rogeri et al., Biomass 5, 79 (2025) (https://repositorio.usp.br/directbitstream/e13d96b7-270e-47ff-9e70-d0ad433e30c0/biomass-05-00079_%281%29.pdf): covered lagoon, CSTR, UASB, AnSTBR hazards (H2S-rich biogas); no yield data used.
