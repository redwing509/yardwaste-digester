# Contributing to Yard-Waste Digester

Contributions are welcome: feedstock data, gas-yield measurements, corrections to the economics, parts substitutions, build photos and notes, safety improvements, and control code.

Open an issue to discuss your contribution, or fork the repository and open a pull request targeting `main`. Include a clear description, why the change helps, and its sources or test evidence (measured data, logs, or a working build). Do not submit secrets, personal information, or material you don't have permission to share. Identify authorship and any third-party licenses.

Every outside submission starts as **submitted / unvalidated**. The owner, @redwing509, reviews it and decides whether it belongs in the project. Corrections may be requested, and inclusion can be declined when evidence is incomplete. Approval must apply to the latest changes. Nothing is merged automatically.

`main` requires a pull request, an approving owner review, and resolved review discussions. New changes dismiss stale approvals. Do not request write access to bypass review.

**Safety:** biogas is flammable and can contain toxic hydrogen sulfide. Changes that affect gas handling, pressure relief, or flame arrestors must explain the safety impact and will get extra scrutiny.
